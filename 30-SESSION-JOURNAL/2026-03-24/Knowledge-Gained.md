# Knowledge Gained: 2026-03-24

## Industry Economics (Sourced Data)

- 3,500–5,000 SEC-registered RIAs in $200M–$600M band
- Median RIA: $393M AUM, 8 employees
- Operating margins compressed from 28% (2020) to 18% (2023)
- Compensation = 74% of total expenses
- Tech budgets: 3.8–5% of revenue
- Revenue per client ~$20,000/year for $350M firm
- 51% cite admin time as top barrier to growth

## AI Adoption (Schwab Study, Jan 2026)

- 63% of RIAs use AI (doubled since 2023)
- Only 6–7% fully integrate AI into strategy
- ChatGPT 70%, Copilot 29% usage
- 71% use for admin, 38% marketing, 31% correspondence
- 78% lack written AI policies
- 44% of compliance pros have no formal testing of AI outputs
- Lisa Salvi: "Gap between early adopters and everyone else is widening"

## Regulatory Clarity

- SEC Predictive Data Analytics rule WITHDRAWN June 12, 2025
- Commissioner Peirce called it "broadly and clumsily" written
- Current posture: principles-based, have adequate policies
- Two RIAs fined $400K combined for "AI washing" (March 2024) — misrepresenting AI, not using AI
- 2026 exam priorities explicitly target AI oversight

## Market Positioning Data

- Fractional executive market: $5.7B+, 14% CAGR
- LinkedIn fractional profiles: 2,000 (2022) → 110,000+ (2024)
- Fractional CFO demand surged 310% since 2020
- Gartner: 30%+ of midsize enterprises will have fractional executive by 2027
- No specialized fractional AI officer for RIA industry exists

## AI Failure Rate Data

- 95% of GenAI pilots fail P&L impact (MIT NANDA)
- 80%+ AI projects fail production (RAND — 2x non-AI IT failure)
- 42% abandoned AI initiatives in 2025 (S&P Global)
- DIY: 33% success. Vendor-led: 67% success (MIT)
