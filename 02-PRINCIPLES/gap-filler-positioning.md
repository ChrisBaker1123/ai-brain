## Principle
Advisors already have dedicated systems for portfolio management, financial planning, tax analysis, and risk assessment. They will NOT use AI for those functions. They WILL use AI for the gaps between systems: meeting prep, follow-up emails, CRM notes, client communication, prospecting, and content creation. Advisor Intelligence fills those gaps.

## Derived From
- [[when-someone-needs-help]] — The gap IS the problem advisors need help with
- [[stick-to-what-you-know]] — Communication and content are the underserved areas

## How It Applies
Never position as replacement for eMoney, Holistiplan, Riskalyze, or any planning/analysis software. Always "complements" and "fills the gap." Templates that overlap with existing systems hurt credibility.
