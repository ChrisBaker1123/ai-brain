# Advisor Intelligence Consulting Agreement — DLK Investment Management

**Date**: March 24, 2026
**Client**: DLK Investment Management, LLC ("Client")
**Consultant**: Advisor Intelligence / Christopher Baker ("Consultant")

---

## 1. Scope of Services

Consultant will provide fractional AI consulting services ("Fractional AI Officer") to Client, including:

**Onboarding (Month 1)**:
- Individual interviews with each team member (15-20 minutes) to assess workflow needs
- Complete audit of Client's technology stack (Salesforce, Schwab Advisor Center, intelliflo redblack, Microsoft 365)
- Draft AI Usage Policy for Chief Compliance Officer review
- Design and build of three (3) custom AI-powered workflows:
  1. Advisor Prospecting Intelligence System (for Don Dempster — M&A/succession planning intelligence)
  2. New Client Onboarding Sequence in Salesforce (for Mark Halby — automated task generation and pipeline tracking)
  3. Client Review Cycle Automation in Salesforce (for Mark Halby — 90-day review scheduling, draft emails, meeting briefs)
- Group training session for all team members
- Month 1 deliverable report

**Ongoing Retainer (Month 2+)**:
- One (1) new workflow build or significant enhancement per month
- AI tool landscape monitoring: new tools, updates, recommendations
- Salesforce maintenance and optimization
- Ad-hoc support via phone, email, and text (unlimited)
- Monthly deliverable report documenting work completed and metrics
- Quarterly business review with firm leadership

## 2. Fees

| Component | Amount | Due |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| Setup/onboarding fee | $2,500 | Upon signing |
| Monthly retainer | $1,250 | 1st of each month, beginning Month 2 |

Payment via check or ACH. Net 15 days.

**Year 1 total**: $17,500 ($2,500 + $1,250 × 12)

## 3. Term and Termination

- **Initial term**: Month-to-month (no minimum commitment)
- **Cancellation**: Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice
- **Transition**: Upon termination, Consultant will provide complete documentation of all workflows built, configurations made, and transition assistance for 14 days at no additional cost

## 4. Confidentiality

- Consultant will not disclose Client's proprietary business information, client data, or internal processes to third parties
- Consultant will not share client personally identifiable information (PII) or account data outside Client's systems
- All work product (workflows, Salesforce configurations, documentation, scripts) belongs to Client
- Consultant may reference Client as a case study for marketing purposes with prior written approval from Client

## 5. Data Handling

- Consultant accesses Client systems only through dedicated user accounts with role-appropriate permissions
- No client PII is exported to Consultant's personal systems
- Advisor prospecting system uses only publicly available SEC data
- Detailed data handling protocol: see attached [[20-CLIENTS/DLK/Compliance/Data-Handling-Protocol|Data Handling Protocol]]
- Client retains full ownership and control of all data at all times

## 6. Compliance

- Consultant will deliver a draft AI Usage Policy within the first week of engagement for CCO review
- All AI-generated client communications are reviewed and approved by Client personnel before sending
- Consultant maintains awareness of current SEC/FINRA AI guidance and incorporates into all workflows
- Consultant is not a registered investment adviser and does not provide investment advice
- Consultant does not have trading authority on any Client accounts

## 7. Access Requirements

Client will provide:
- [ ] Dedicated Salesforce user account with appropriate permissions (admin recommended for Flow building)
- [ ] Introduction to each team member for initial interviews
- [ ] Access to Salesforce documentation (edition, current configuration)
- [ ] List of current tools and integrations

Consultant will:
- [ ] Use only the dedicated account provided
- [ ] Log all access and changes
- [ ] Never access accounts outside defined scope
- [ ] Store credentials in encrypted password manager (never in plain text)

## 8. Limitation of Liability

Consultant's total liability shall not exceed the total fees paid under this agreement in the preceding 12 months. Consultant is not liable for investment outcomes, market performance, or decisions made using AI-generated content.

## 9. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.
