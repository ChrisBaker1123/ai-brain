## Principle
Start narrow with maximum depth, then widen. Financial advisors first, then broader professional services. San Diego first, then national. Templates first, then AI installations. Each expansion builds on existing trust and proven capability.

## Derived From
- [[stick-to-what-you-know]] — Build from a position of knowledge
- [[trust-before-money]] — Trust is earned in niches before it scales

## How It Applies
Resist the urge to build for "everyone." The Phase 1 → 2 → 3 strategy is the macro expression of this principle. At the tactical level: target RIAs before wirehouses, target San Diego before national.
