## Principle
Most tech founders don't understand finance. Most financial advisors don't understand tech. Cri understands both. He can sit with an advisor and understand their compliance fears, workflow pain, and client communication challenges. Then he can build the technology that solves it. That's the moat.

## Derived From
- [[stick-to-what-you-know]] — The intersection of two knowledge domains is where to build

## How It Applies
Every product decision, marketing message, and sales conversation should demonstrate fluency in both worlds. Content should show advisor-level understanding of workflows alongside technical competence.
