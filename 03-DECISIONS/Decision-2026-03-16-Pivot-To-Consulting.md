# Decision: Pivot from Toolkit-First to Consulting-First Positioning

**Date**: 2026-03-16
**Decision maker**: Christopher Baker
**Advisor**: Dan Skiles (VP Advisor Relationships, Altruist)

---

## The Decision

Restructure Advisor Intelligence from a $50/month AI toolkit SaaS into a monthly AI consulting retainer ($497-$1,997/month) where Cri is the product. The toolkit still exists as an included resource within the consulting relationship, but the primary offer is the human relationship: monthly AI audit, check-in calls, custom recommendations, on-demand support, and ongoing guidance.

---

## Why

Dan Skiles gave the key insight: the real business isn't the toolkit. It's Cri himself as a consultant who prevents financial advisors from falling behind on AI. The analogy: in the early tech era, businesses didn't hire full-time IT departments. They outsourced to someone who came in periodically, checked the systems, fixed problems, gave recommendations. That's what Advisor Intelligence becomes for AI.

### Supporting research:

1. **Wirehouses are a dead end for product sales.** Morgan Stanley has 98% AI adoption with OpenAI partnership. Wells Fargo has Tachyon. FINRA rules block outside technology vendors. A $50/month toolkit cannot penetrate wirehouse compliance.

2. **RIAs need a guide, not another tool.** 63% of RIAs use AI but only 10% strategically. They're overwhelmed by 300+ software options. They don't need another tool — they need someone who understands their practice and can cut through the noise.

3. **Monthly consulting revenue is what acquirers value.** $50/month SaaS with 4 users = $200 MRR. 10 consulting clients at $997/month = $9,970 MRR. Same effort, 50x the revenue.

4. **The moat is Cri, not the toolkit.** Anyone can build templates. No one else grew up in an advisory household, builds AI systems, and can sit across from a $500M advisor and speak their language. The human is the differentiator.

5. **This accelerates the 3-phase plan.** Monthly calls build deeper trust than a $50 SaaS product. By the time Phase 2 (AI installations) arrives, the relationships are already deep enough.

## What Changes

| Aspect | Before | After |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Primary offer** | $50/month AI toolkit | $497-$1,997/month consulting retainer |
| **What we sell** | Software access | Human relationship + expertise |
| **Toolkit role** | The product | An included resource |
| **Website job** | Sell product signups | Get discovery calls booked |
| **Revenue model** | High-volume, low-price SaaS | Low-volume, high-value consulting |
| **Sales process** | Self-serve signup | Discovery call → needs assessment → proposal |
| **Target market** | All advisors | Independent RIAs (primary), wirehouse personal coaching (limited) |
| **CTA** | "Get Started Free" | "Book a Free Discovery Call" |

## What Doesn't Change

- The toolkit (68 templates, documents, guides, AI Coach) still exists
- The technology platform still works
- The compliance-aware positioning still applies
- The gap-filler insight is still valid
- The 3-phase career plan (AI consulting → AI architecture → financial advisor) is unchanged — strengthened
- Phone number, personal support, same-person-everywhere brand

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Cri can't serve many clients at once | Start with 10 clients. Scale is Phase 2's problem. |
| Price resistance at $997/month | Carson charges the same. Lead with ROI: "save 5+ hours/week." |
| No track record yet | First 1-2 clients at discount/pilot. Build case studies fast. |
| Toolkit feels devalued | Position as "included" not "free." It's a $600/year resource they get as a bonus. |
| Consulting doesn't scale | That's by design. Phase 2 (installations) scales. Phase 1 builds trust. |
