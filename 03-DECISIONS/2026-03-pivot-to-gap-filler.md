## Decision
Reorganize from 11 planning-function categories to 8 workflow categories. Remove 13 templates that overlap with existing systems. Reframe 18 templates from "analysis" to "communication/meeting prep." Add ~19 new gap-filler templates. Position as "the AI layer for everything your existing systems don't cover."

## Context
In-person coaching sessions with advisors at Morgan Stanley, Wells Fargo, and independent RIAs revealed: advisors will not use AI for tasks where they already have regulated, monitored systems. Having "Roth Conversion Analysis" on the platform signals we don't understand their world.

## Rationale
Templates for analysis functions hurt credibility. The genuine gap is communication and workflow: meeting prep, follow-ups, emails, prospecting, content creation. 43% of advisors already use AI for email drafting (Horsesmouth 2025). No funded competitor fills this gap.

## Implications
- New category architecture: meeting_prep, follow_up_crm, client_emails, client_education, prospecting_growth, content_creation, new_client_onboarding, practice_operations
- Landing page, pricing, about, compare pages all need messaging updates
- Demo flow changes from planning-function showcase to workflow showcase
- Brand voice rule: never position as replacement for existing systems
