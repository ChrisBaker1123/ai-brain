# Altitude CRM

> Last Updated: 2026-03-24. Sources: Altitude website, industry reviews.

## Overview

Advisor-specific CRM with "Pathfinder+" AI assistant built in. Competes with Salesforce, Redtail, and Wealthbox in the advisor CRM space.

## Features

- Advisor-specific CRM
- Pathfinder+ AI assistant (built into the CRM)
- Client relationship management
- Task and workflow automation
- Financial account tracking

## Relevance to DLK

None — DLK is on Salesforce. Altitude is relevant as context for firms evaluating CRM changes.
