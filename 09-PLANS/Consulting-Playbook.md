# Consulting Playbook — Month-by-Month Engagement

> How a consulting engagement works from first call to ongoing retainer.

---

## Pre-Engagement: The Sales Process

### Step 1: Discovery Call (Free, 15-20 min)
- Understand the practice: size, AUM, client count, team structure
- Identify pain points: "What's your biggest frustration with AI/technology right now?"
- Assess AI maturity: what tools they use, what they've tried, what failed
- Listen 80%, talk 20%
- If it's a fit, schedule the needs assessment

### Step 2: Needs Assessment (Free, 45-60 min)
- Deep dive into current workflows, tech stack, daily pain points
- Live demo of 2-3 templates relevant to THEIR practice
- Show (don't tell) how AI can save time on tasks THEY named
- Ask: "If we could save you 5 hours a week on client communications, what would that be worth?"
- By the end, they should understand the value. The proposal confirms it.

### Step 3: Proposal (Sent within 48 hours)
- 2-3 pages max
- Structure: Problem summary → Proposed solution → Timeline → Deliverables → Investment → Next steps
- Reference THEIR specific pain points from the conversation
- Include the recommended tier with clear deliverables
- "This proposal confirms what we discussed, not a sales pitch"

### Step 4: Close & Kickoff
- Address final questions
- Signature + payment
- Schedule Month 1 kickoff call within 1 week
- Send welcome email with toolkit access credentials

---

## Month 1: Onboarding — The AI Audit

### Week 1: Kickoff + Data Gathering
- 60-min kickoff call: confirm goals, introduce engagement structure
- Send technology audit questionnaire:
  - Current tools (CRM, planning, portfolio, compliance, communication)
  - AI tools in use (ChatGPT, Copilot, meeting transcription, etc.)
  - Biggest time sinks in a typical week
  - Client communication workflows
  - Team structure and roles
- Grant toolkit access (templates, documents, guides)

### Week 2-3: AI Audit
- Review questionnaire responses
- Analyze their tech stack for gaps and redundancies
- Assess AI maturity level (experimentation → strategic)
- Identify top 3 quick wins (tasks where AI saves immediate time)
- Identify top 3 strategic opportunities (longer-term workflow improvements)

### Week 4: Roadmap Delivery
- 60-min call to present findings
- Deliver: **AI Audit Report** (current state assessment)
- Deliver: **90-Day AI Roadmap** (prioritized action plan)
  - Quick wins (implement this month)
  - Medium-term improvements (next 30-60 days)
  - Strategic initiatives (60-90 days)
- Get client buy-in on priorities
- Schedule Month 2 check-in

### Month 1 Deliverables:
- [x] Kickoff call
- [x] Technology audit questionnaire completed
- [x] AI Audit Report
- [x] 90-Day AI Roadmap
- [x] Toolkit access granted
- [x] First quick win implemented

## Month 2+: Ongoing Engagement

### Monthly Check-In Call (30-60 min depending on tier)
- Review progress against roadmap
- Address new pain points or questions
- Demo new AI tools or techniques relevant to their practice
- Update recommendations based on what's working
- Plan next month's focus

### Monthly AI Brief (1-page, delivered by email)
- What changed in AI this month (filtered for what matters to advisors)
- New tools worth evaluating
- Regulatory updates related to AI
- Template/workflow recommendations based on their evolving needs

### On-Demand Support (email/phone/text)
- Quick questions between calls: "Can AI help me with X?"
- Template customization requests
- Tool recommendations
- Troubleshooting AI outputs

### Toolkit Updates
- New templates and prompts added as Cri builds them
- Custom templates created for specific client needs
- Updated compliance references as regulations evolve

---

## Quarterly Business Review (QBR)

### Every 90 Days:
- 60-90 min deep session
- Review AI ROI: time saved, workflows improved, client satisfaction impact
- Update the roadmap for the next quarter
- Adjust strategy based on what's working and what isn't
- Team training session if applicable (Tier 2+)
- Discuss new tools, industry trends, competitive landscape

### QBR Deliverables:
- Updated AI Roadmap (next 90 days)
- ROI summary (hours saved, workflows improved)
- Recommendations for next quarter
- Team training materials (if applicable)

## Engagement Milestones

| Month | Milestone | Client Should See |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | AI audit + roadmap complete | Clear picture of where they stand and where to go |
| 2 | First 3 quick wins implemented | Measurable time savings (5+ hours/week) |
| 3 | First QBR | ROI documented, roadmap updated |
| 6 | Full workflow integration | AI embedded in daily operations, not just experimentation |
| 9 | Team-wide adoption | All advisors using AI effectively (not just the champion) |
| 12 | Annual review | Full year ROI, strategic AI roadmap for Year 2 |

---

## Team Training Sessions (Tier 2 quarterly, Tier 3 monthly)

### Format:
- 30-45 min virtual session with the advisory team
- Hands-on: bring a real task, build the AI workflow together
- Record the session for team members who can't attend
- Follow up with written guide + templates

### Topics (rotate based on need):
- "Copy-Paste AI 101: Your first meeting prep template"
- "Client email workflows: from blank page to polished draft in 2 minutes"
- "CRM notes in 30 seconds: post-meeting AI workflow"
- "LinkedIn content: creating advisor-appropriate posts with AI"
- "Market volatility response: rapid client communication"
- "New AI tools review: what's worth trying this quarter"

## Client Success Metrics (What We Track)

| Metric | How We Measure | Target |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| Time saved per week | Self-reported + workflow comparison | 5-10 hours/week |
| Templates used | Toolkit analytics | 10+ templates/month |
| AI maturity score | Custom assessment (1-10 scale) | Improve by 3+ points in 6 months |
| Client satisfaction | Quarterly check-in rating | 8+/10 |
| Referral willingness | "Would you refer a colleague?" | Yes by month 3 |
