# Content Inspiration Research

#personal #brand #content #research

> 10 creators who blend personal brand + professional expertise. Models for Cri's Instagram pivot. Updated March 2026.

---

## What I'm Looking For

Creators who are the same person in content as they are in real life. People who blend personal authenticity with professional credibility. Preferably in the 10K–500K follower range (accessible models, not unreachable celebrities). Mix of finance, tech, AI, and entrepreneurship.

## The Playbook (What I'm Taking From All 10)

1. **Give away the playbook, sell the implementation** (Hormozi, Isenberg, Rachitsky)
2. **90-second AI explainers for advisors** (Winfield format + my niche)
3. **Tell the founder story in real time** (Lieberman parallel — college founder)
4. **Newsletter as product** (Rachitsky, Bloom — build an audience that pays)
5. **Finance + personal brand = lucrative** (Bloom proof of concept)
6. **You can be credible at 29K followers** (Friedman proof)
7. **Operational AI, not theoretical AI** (Woods framing — "here's how to use it")
8. **Same person everywhere** — every creator on this list is authentic across platforms
9. **Young advisor personal brand** (Yarnway — the Phase 3 model)
10. **San Diego is part of the brand** (Schulte — local identity differentiates)
