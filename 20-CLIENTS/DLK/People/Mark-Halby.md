## What He Wants from AI

### Primary: Salesforce Automation (WF2 + WF3)

Mark's Salesforce is massively underutilized. He knows it can do more. His exact wish list from transcript:

**New Client Onboarding (WF2)**:
- New prospect created → auto-generate task list:
  - Send disclosure documents
  - Set up in billing system
  - Assign to investment model (Brian)
  - Enter account data for trading (Brian)
  - Add to newsletter distribution
  - Schedule introductory review meeting
  - Set 30/60/90 day calendar reminders
  - Send welcome email (draft for review)
- Dashboard: "Where are we in the process? What's already been done and what still needs to be done?"
- Pipeline visibility on his homepage
- **System**: Webhook on new prospect creation → Auto-populate tasks in Salesforce → Homepage shows progress

**Review Cycle Automation (WF3)**:
- **Setup**: Track last review date and next review date for each client
- **60 days before**: Auto-draft email ("It's time for your periodic review")
- **45 days before**: Client notes review — any life events logged since last review? Include in next meeting prep
- **30 days before**: If no response to 60-day email, escalate to calendar task (Mark to call/email)
- **Pre-meeting** (1 week before): Generate one-page client brief:
  - Last meeting notes summary
  - Account value change
  - Any life events logged (kids' graduations, moves, job changes)
  - Upcoming milestones (college starts in 3 months, etc.)
- **Post-meeting**: AI processes notes (Mark dictates or types) into CRM update, creates follow-up tasks, drafts recap email to client
- **Homepage**: Upcoming reviews for next 90 days, which are in next 30 days, trigger review emails automatically

**Exact quote from Mark**: "Best case scenario, it would be a sequence where you start, a new client comes on and then it gives you everything it needs to do, does everything it can."

Mark's current daily routine (from transcript):
1. 7:00 AM: Log into Schwab Advisor Center → check alerts, maintenance items, cash transfers
2. 7:15 AM: Log into intelliflo redblack → check drift report (5%+ threshold, which triggers rebalancing)
3. 7:30 AM: Send rebalancing list to Brian ("rebalance this account, free up cash for this one, move X to Y")

- Salesforce new tasks and reviews due in next 30 days

**Delivery**: Daily 7 AM email or dashboard:

- Ready-to-forward rebalancing list for Brian
