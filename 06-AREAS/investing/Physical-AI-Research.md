#personal #investing #research

> Comprehensive company research for a higher-risk physical AI portfolio. Real tickers, real data, real risk assessments. Updated March 2026.

## Thesis Statement

The next wave of outsized AI returns comes from putting intelligence into the physical world. LLMs are priced in. The companies deploying AI into robots, vehicles, warehouses, construction sites, and surgical suites are addressing the largest possible market — global labor — and the convergence of foundation models, computer vision, and robotics is reaching deployment threshold in 2025–2030.

---

## Key Trends Driving Physical AI

1. **Humanoid robot race**: Tesla, Figure, Apptronik, Agility, Boston Dynamics all racing to scale production
2. **Warehouse automation inflection**: Symbotic just turned profitable with a $22.4B backlog
3. **Autonomous trucking deployment**: Aurora targeting 200+ commercial trucks by end of 2026
4. **Construction labor crisis**: 650,000+ construction worker shortage in the US, pushing automation adoption
5. **NVIDIA's physical AI platform**: Isaac Sim + Omniverse + Cosmos create the developer ecosystem
6. **ABB robotics spinoff**: Planned Q2 2026, could unlock significant value in industrial robotics
7. **New ETFs**: WPAI (physical AI ETF) launched February 2026, signaling institutional interest

## Portfolio Construction Ideas

For a higher-risk, physical AI-focused portfolio at age 20:

### Core Holdings (50% of allocation)
- **NVDA** — the infrastructure play, every physical AI company depends on
- **GOOGL** — broadest physical AI exposure (Waymo + DeepMind)
- **SYM** — proven warehouse automation with massive backlog
- **ABB** — robotics spinoff catalyst, blue-chip base

### Growth Holdings (30% of allocation)
- **HYMTF** — best risk/reward in humanoid robots (Boston Dynamics upside)
- **TER** — dual exposure (cobots + chip testing)
- **TRMB** — construction automation leader
- **AUR** — autonomous trucking first-mover

### Speculative Holdings (15% of allocation)
- **MBLY** — deep value ADAS play, 101% analyst upside
- **OSK** — autonomous municipal vehicles
- **FBRKF** — tiny position, construction robot moonshot

### IPO Watch List (5% cash reserved)
- **Figure AI** — wait for IPO, don't chase private valuation
- **Apptronik** — Google DeepMind partnership is the signal
- **Agility Robotics** — first commercial humanoid deployment

### ETF Option
- **WPAI** — if you want a single-ticker physical AI bet, this is it
