# AI Implementation Failure Rates

> Last Updated: 2026-03-24. Sources: MIT NANDA 2025, RAND Corporation, S&P Global, Fortune, HBR March 2026, Advisor360, Lucid Software, Zylo 2025.

## Headline Statistics

| Metric | Value | Source |
|--------|-------|--------|
| GenAI pilots failing to deliver P&L impact | **95%** | MIT NANDA 2025 |
| AI projects failing to reach production | **80%+** | RAND Corporation (2x failure rate of non-AI IT) |
| Companies abandoning most AI initiatives (2025) | **42%** | S&P Global (up from 17% in 2024) |
| DIY AI success rate | **~33%** | MIT |
| Vendor-led AI success rate | **~67%** | MIT (2x better than DIY) |
| Orgs reporting positive ROI on AI | **34%** | Lucid Software |
| Enterprise SaaS seats going unused | **53%** | Zylo 2025 |

## Why AI Projects Fail

HBR March 2026 identified **seven structural frictions** preventing AI from scaling:
1. Data quality and accessibility
2. Integration with existing workflows
3. Change management and adoption
4. Unclear success metrics
5. Talent and skills gaps
6. Governance and compliance uncertainty
7. Vendor fragmentation

## Financial Services Specific

- 66% of advisors say firm's technology needs improvement (Advisor360)
- Top shortcomings: lack of AI capabilities (39%), poor integration (37%)
- Only 50% of firm technology is integrated (Ezra Group)
- Average firm has 300+ software options across 48 categories to choose from
