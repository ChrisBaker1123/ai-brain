# Future Outlook — 12, 24, 36 Month Assessment

> Honest assessment of viability, threats, and evolution.

---

## 12 Months (March 2027)

**Does the business still exist?** Yes, and it's stronger.

**Why**: The AI landscape changes faster than advisors can track. In the next 12 months, we'll see agentic AI (tools that execute tasks, not just draft content), deeper CRM integrations, new regulatory guidance from SEC/FINRA on AI use, and continued tool proliferation. Every month brings new tools, new capabilities, and new compliance questions. That's exactly what drives demand for a consultant who translates and implements.

**What changes**: The toolkit component (copy-paste templates) becomes less differentiated as AI tools get smarter and competitors emerge. But the CONSULTING component — the audit, the roadmap, the monthly check-in, the accumulated knowledge of each practice — becomes MORE valuable because there's MORE to evaluate and implement.

**Risk**: If a well-funded competitor (Focal AI raised $5M, F2 Strategy is PE-backed) launches an advisor-specific AI consulting practice, they'd have more resources. Mitigation: Cri's relationships and accumulated practice knowledge create switching costs that money can't buy.

**Revenue target**: 8-10 clients, $6K-$10K MRR.

---

## 24 Months (March 2028)

**Does the business still exist?** It evolves into Phase 2.

**What happens**: Cri graduates SDSU (Class of 2028). The consulting practice has 12-18 months of client data, relationships, and proven results. This is the natural inflection point for Phase 2: AI architecture installations.

**Phase 2 in practice**: "You've been getting my monthly advice for a year. Now let me actually build and install the systems." This means custom AI workflows, CRM integrations, automated meeting prep pipelines, personalized client communication systems. Project fees of $5K-$50K per installation, layered on top of ongoing consulting retainers.

**The toolkit's role**: The template library becomes the foundation for installed systems. Templates evolve from "copy-paste" to "automated" — running inside the advisor's own infrastructure.

**Risk**: Technology shifts could make current AI tools obsolete. Mitigation: The consulting model is tool-agnostic. When tools change, Cri helps clients migrate. That IS the service.

**Revenue target**: 15 retainer clients ($12-15K MRR) + 3-5 installation projects per year ($15K-$100K project revenue).

---

## 36 Months (March 2029)

**Does the business still exist?** It's transitioning to Phase 3.

**What happens**: By year 3, Cri has deep relationships with 20+ advisory practices. He understands their clients, their workflows, their growth plans. This is the trust capital that enables Phase 3: becoming a financial advisor himself.

**Phase 3 in practice**: "I've been your AI consultant and technology partner for 2-3 years. You trust me. I'm now building my own advisory practice — and I'd like to manage some of the wealth I've been helping you serve better."

**Risk**: The transition from consultant to advisor could feel like a betrayal to existing clients. Mitigation: Frame it as expansion, not abandonment. "I'm adding financial advising to what I do, not stopping the AI consulting."

## What Might Kill This Business

| Threat | Probability | Severity | Mitigation |
|--------|------------|----------|-----------|
| AI tools become so easy advisors don't need help | Low (12-24 mo) | High | The "easy" tools still need evaluation, implementation, and compliance review. The need shifts from "help me use AI" to "help me choose the right AI and keep up." |
| Well-funded competitor enters advisor AI consulting | Medium | Medium | First-mover relationship advantage. Switching costs from accumulated practice knowledge. Price competitively. |
| Regulatory crackdown on AI in advisory | Low | Medium | Positions Cri as the compliance-aware guide through the crackdown. Actually increases demand. |
| Cri's time constraints (full-time student + consultant) | High | Medium | Cap clients at 10-12 until graduation. Systematize deliverables (monthly brief, toolkit updates). |
| Economic downturn reduces advisory spending | Medium | Low | $997/mo is a rounding error for a $365M AUM firm generating $3.6M/year. Consulting is the last thing cut if ROI is documented. |

---

## What Keeps This Alive

1. **The AI landscape changes monthly.** This is structural, not cyclical. As long as AI evolves, advisors need a translator.
2. **Accumulated knowledge is irreplaceable.** The longer the relationship, the harder to replace.
3. **The trust funnel is the product.** Each month builds toward Phase 2 (installations) and Phase 3 (advising). Even if the consulting revenue plateaus, the relationships compound.
4. **New advisors enter the industry every year.** The market doesn't shrink — it refreshes. Young advisors starting practices will need AI guidance too.
