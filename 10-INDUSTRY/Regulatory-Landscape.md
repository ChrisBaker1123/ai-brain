# Regulatory Landscape — AI in Financial Services

> Last Updated: 2026-03-24. Sources: SEC.gov, Sidley Austin, WilmerHale, Morrison Foerster, Kitces, DLA Piper, Goodwin, FINRA 2025 Annual Regulatory Oversight Report.

## Key Takeaway

**NO AI-specific rules for RIAs.** Technology-neutral framework applies. The SEC uses a principles-based approach: have adequate policies and procedures in place.

## SEC

### Predictive Data Analytics Rule — WITHDRAWN

- SEC Predictive Data Analytics rule was **withdrawn June 12, 2025** under Chair Atkins
- Commissioner Peirce criticized original proposal as "broadly and clumsily" written
- Current SEC posture: principles-based, adequate policies and procedures required

### 2026 Examination Priorities

SEC 2026 exam priorities **explicitly target AI oversight**:
- AI use in investment advice and portfolio management
- Marketing Rule compliance for AI-generated content
- Cybersecurity risks from AI tool adoption
- Books and records for AI-assisted work
- Vendor due diligence for AI service providers

### Key Rules Affecting AI

| Rule | Requirement | AI Implication |
|------|------------|----------------|
| **Marketing Rule 206(4)-1** | No misleading claims in marketing | Compliance review of ALL AI-drafted client communications |
| **Books and Records 204-2** | Retain business communications | Must capture AI-generated content that becomes client communications |
| **Fiduciary Duty** | Adviser must exercise independent judgment | AI cannot override or replace fiduciary obligation — human review required |
| **Rule 206(4)-7** | Written policies addressing risks | Must include AI-specific risks in compliance policies |

### Enforcement Actions

- Two RIAs fined **$400,000 combined** in March 2024 for "AI washing" — claiming AI capabilities they didn't have
- SEC scrutinizing marketing claims about AI tools
- No fines yet for *using* AI, only for *misrepresenting* AI capabilities

### Practical Compliance Checklist (for Advisor Intelligence engagements)

- [ ] SOC 2 compliance for any tools handling client data
- [ ] No model training on client data — verify with every vendor
- [ ] Audit trails for all AI-generated content
- [ ] Human review before any AI output reaches clients
- [ ] Vendor due diligence documentation for every AI tool
- [ ] Record retention covering AI prompts, outputs, and logs
- [ ] Written AI usage policy (our Month 1 deliverable)

## FINRA

### AI-Related Guidance (2025 Annual Regulatory Oversight Report)

- Supervisory requirements apply to AI-generated communications
- Record retention for AI interactions
- Suitability/best interest obligations unaffected by AI assistance
- AI tools used by registered reps must be disclosed and supervised

### Wirehouse Implications

- **Rule 3270**: Advisors cannot hire outside consultants without firm approval
- **Rule 3110**: All technology must be supervised by the firm
- **Off-channel crackdown**: $63M in fines in Jan 2025 alone for unapproved tools
- FINRA restrictions make it **near-impossible** for outside consultants to build systems at wirehouses
- This is why Advisor Intelligence limits wirehouse engagements to personal coaching only

## State Regulations

### California

- **CCPA**: Consumer data privacy rights (may apply if handling client data)
- **CalOPPA**: Online privacy requirements
- **Technology consultant licensing**: No specific license required for AI consulting — standard business license sufficient (needs verification)

## Practical Implications for Advisor Intelligence

1. **Every workflow must have human review** — AI drafts, advisor decides
2. **AI Usage Policy is a deliverable** — Not optional, it's compliance infrastructure
3. **Vendor due diligence is required** — Document every tool we recommend (see [[20-CLIENTS/DLK/Compliance/Vendor-Due-Diligence|DLK Vendor Due Diligence]])
4. **Record retention must cover AI** — Prompts, outputs, logs stored in CRM or document system
5. **Marketing content gets extra scrutiny** — CCO review before any publication
6. **No investment advice from AI** — Clear boundary in every engagement
7. **"Compliance-aware, not compliance-approved"** — We help build the framework, the CCO approves it (see [[02-PRINCIPLES/compliance-aware-not-approved|Principle]])

## Key Quotes for Sales

> "There are no AI-specific rules. But the SEC expects you to have policies covering AI, just like any other technology risk." — paraphrased from WilmerHale guidance

> "The firms that will get in trouble aren't the ones using AI — they're the ones using AI without policies." — industry consensus
