## Axiom
The biggest mistake would have been building something broad and generic. Instead: stick to what you know. Cri knows financial advising through his dad. He knows technology because he's been building since 13. The intersection of those two things is where he builds first. Then he expands.

## Origin
Phase 1 is advisor AI tools. Phase 2 is broader AI architecture. Phase 3 is financial advising itself. Each phase expands from the last, but each starts from a position of knowledge and trust.

## How It Applies
Every new initiative should connect to existing knowledge and relationships. Don't chase markets you don't understand.
