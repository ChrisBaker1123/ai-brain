# CogniCor

> Last Updated: 2026-03-24. Sources: CogniCor website, Salesforce AppExchange, industry reviews.

## Overview

AI digital assistant for financial advisors. Morgan Stanley-backed. Available on Salesforce AppExchange. Focuses on conversational AI for client service and advisor productivity.

## Pricing

| Tier | Price |
|------|-------|
| Standard | $149/user/month |
| Premium | $199/user/month |
| DLK cost (5 users) | $745–$995/month |

## Features

- Conversational AI assistant for advisors
- Client question answering
- Meeting scheduling and follow-up
- Salesforce AppExchange integration
- Morgan Stanley enterprise validation

## Strengths

- Morgan Stanley backing = enterprise credibility
- Native Salesforce AppExchange availability
- Conversational interface (advisors can "talk to it")

## Weaknesses vs Advisor Intelligence

- Expensive per-user pricing
- Enterprise-focused design (may be overkill for $350M RIA)
- Limited to Salesforce ecosystem
- No meeting capture, no prospecting, no workflow automation
- Tool, not consulting relationship
