## Core Thesis

The next wave of AI value creation isn't in language models — it's in **physical AI implementation**. LLMs are already priced into the market. The companies that will generate outsized returns are the ones putting AI into the physical world: robots that build, vehicles that drive, warehouses that run themselves, factories that optimize in real-time.

I want a higher-risk portfolio focused on this thesis. Not meme stocks, not hype plays — real companies building real systems that automate physical work.

---

## Why Physical AI

### 1. LLMs Are Already Priced In
NVIDIA, Microsoft, Google, Meta — the "AI winners" are already valued at their AI premium. The market for text and image generation is well-understood. Physical AI is where the pricing gap exists.

### 2. Labor Is the Biggest Market
Software automates information work. Physical AI automates labor. The global labor market is worth trillions. Companies that can replace or augment physical labor with robots and autonomous systems are addressing the largest possible market.

### 3. The Convergence Is Happening Now
Foundation models are getting good enough to power physical systems. Computer vision, spatial reasoning, manipulation planning — these capabilities are reaching the threshold where robots can operate in unstructured environments. 2025–2030 is the deployment window.

### 4. It Connects to What I Know
Advisor Intelligence is an AI product for a specific industry. Physical AI is the same pattern at a larger scale: technology that automates what humans currently do manually, deployed into specific industry verticals. I understand the playbook.
