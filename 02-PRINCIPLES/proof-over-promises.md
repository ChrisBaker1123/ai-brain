## Principle
Show the work, not just the results. Demonstrate value through specificity ("68 templates across 8 workflow categories") rather than hype ("revolutionary AI toolkit"). Never fabricate metrics, testimonials, or social proof. Educational content over promotional content in every channel.

## Derived From
- [[build-dont-talk]] — Build it, then show it working
- [[be-the-same-person-everywhere]] — Honesty is the brand

## How It Applies
Landing page uses real numbers, not vague claims. Blog posts teach something, not just promote. LinkedIn posts show behind-the-scenes building, not just polished results.
