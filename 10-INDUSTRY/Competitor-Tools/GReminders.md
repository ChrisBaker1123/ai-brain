# GReminders

> Last Updated: 2026-03-24. Sources: GReminders website, advisor community.

## Overview

Scheduling automation platform with AI notetaking for financial advisors. Low cost, focused on appointment scheduling and reminders with meeting notes as an add-on.

## Pricing

| Tier | Price |
|------|-------|
| Basic scheduling | Free |
| Full features | ~$8/month |

## Features

- Automated appointment scheduling
- Meeting reminders (SMS, email)
- AI meeting notes (add-on)
- Calendar integration
- CRM sync (basic)

## Relevance to DLK

Low-to-medium. Could help with review scheduling automation ([[20-CLIENTS/DLK/Workflows/WF3-Review-Cycle/Spec|WF3]]) but we'd likely build this directly in Salesforce Flows for tighter integration.
