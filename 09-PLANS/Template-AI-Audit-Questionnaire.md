## Production Notes

- **When**: Sent during Week 1 of engagement, due back within 5 business days
- **Format**: Can be sent as a shared doc (Google Docs/Word) or a simple form (Tally, Typeform)
- **Who fills it out**: Each team member individually (for team-wide engagements) or the primary advisor (for solo/essentials tier)
- **What Cri does with it**: Forms the basis of the AI Audit Report and 90-Day Roadmap. Cross-referenced with the Practice Intelligence File.
