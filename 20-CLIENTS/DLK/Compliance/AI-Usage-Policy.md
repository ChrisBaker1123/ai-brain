# DLK Investment Management — Artificial Intelligence Usage Policy

**Effective Date**: [Pending Don's review]
**Last Reviewed**: 2026-03-24 (initial draft)
**Approved By**: Donald O. Dempster, CFA — Chief Compliance Officer
**Next Review**: 12 months from adoption
**Prepared By**: Christopher Baker, Advisor Intelligence

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for the use of artificial intelligence tools at DLK Investment Management, LLC to ensure compliance with SEC and FINRA regulations, protect client data, and maintain the firm's fiduciary standard.

DLK recognizes that AI tools can enhance operational efficiency and client service when used responsibly. This policy provides a framework for approved use while maintaining the firm's compliance obligations and "Disciplined Pursuit of Quality" standard.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all DLK personnel: Donald Dempster, Theodore Kay, Brian Johnson, Thomas Brenner, Mark Halby, and Matthew Van Winkle, as well as any contractors or consultants (including Advisor Intelligence) with access to firm systems.

## 3. Approved AI Tools

| Tool | Approved Use | Restrictions | Approved By |
|------|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Microsoft Copilot (M365)** | Email drafting, document summarization, task management, meeting notes | No client PII in prompts. No investment recommendations. | Don Dempster |
| **Advisor Intelligence Workflows** | Salesforce automation, review cycle management, prospecting intelligence | Operates within Salesforce and firm systems. No client data exported. | Don Dempster |
| **Otter.ai** (pending) | In-person meeting transcription | Review transcripts before saving to CRM. Delete recordings after processing. | Don Dempster |

### Adding New Tools
Any team member wishing to use a new AI tool must:
1. Submit the tool name, vendor, and intended use to Don Dempster
2. Wait for compliance review (vendor due diligence, data handling assessment)
3. Receive written approval before using the tool for any firm business

## 4. Prohibited Uses

AI tools shall **NOT** be used to:

- ❌ Make investment recommendations without advisor review and professional judgment
- ❌ Send client communications without human review and explicit approval
- ❌ Process or transmit: client Social Security Numbers, account numbers, passwords, full dates of birth, or account balances in AI prompts
- ❌ Generate marketing materials without compliance review per SEC Marketing Rule
- ❌ Execute or instruct trades
- ❌ Access or process client data outside of approved firm systems
- ❌ Make suitability or best interest determinations
- ❌ Replace the firm's fiduciary obligations or professional judgment

## 5. Data Classification

| Classification | Examples | AI Usage |
|----------------|----------|----------|
| **Restricted** | Client SSNs, account numbers, passwords, full DOB, tax IDs | **Never enter into any AI tool** |
| **Sensitive** | Client names (first only), general financial situations, account types, life events | Use with caution in approved tools only. Never in combination with restricted data. |
| **Internal** | Firm processes, templates, workflow configurations, internal notes | Permitted with approved tools |
| **Public** | Market data, SEC filings, regulatory text, published research, news | Freely usable |

### Client Data Handling
- AI-generated client communications are drafts until reviewed and approved by the sending advisor
- No client data is stored in third-party AI systems beyond the approved tools listed above
- Advisor Intelligence consultant accesses firm systems only through a dedicated Salesforce user account with logged activity
- The advisor prospecting intelligence system uses only publicly available SEC data — no client data

## 6. Human Review Requirements

| Activity | Review Required By | Before |
|----------|-------------------|--------|
| Client email or letter | Sending advisor (Mark/Tom) | Sending |
| Marketing content | Don Dempster (CCO) | Publishing |
| Financial plan summary | Preparing advisor | Client delivery |
| Compliance document | Don Dempster (CCO) | Filing or distribution |
| Onboarding task list | Workflow owner (Mark) | Acting on auto-generated tasks |
| Review cycle emails | Sending advisor | Sending (emails go to drafts, never auto-sent) |
| Prospecting outreach | Don Dempster | Sending any outreach based on AI intelligence |
| Meeting note summaries | Attending advisor | Saving to CRM |

**Core principle**: AI assists, humans decide. No AI-generated content reaches a client without advisor review.

## 7. Record Retention

| Record Type | Retention Period | Storage |
|-------------|-----------------|---------|
| AI-generated content that becomes client communication | Per firm's existing Books and Records policy (minimum 5 years) | Salesforce + firm archives |
| AI prompts/queries used for client work | 12 months minimum | Firm systems |
| AI tool access logs | 24 months | Tool vendor + firm logs |
| This policy and all revisions | Permanent | Firm compliance files |
| Meeting transcripts processed by AI | Delete recordings after CRM entry; retain CRM notes per standard policy | Salesforce |

## 8. Vendor Due Diligence

For each approved AI vendor, the firm maintains documentation of:
- Company name, headquarters, and primary contact
- Data handling practices: where data is processed, stored, and for how long
- Security certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, etc.)
- Terms of service and privacy policy review date
- Sub-processor list
- Incident response and breach notification commitments
- Whether data is used for model training (must be opt-out or no)

Vendor reviews are conducted annually or upon material change in vendor terms or capabilities.

## 9. Incident Response

If an AI-related data breach, misuse, or error is suspected:
1. **Immediately** notify Don Dempster (CCO)
2. **Document**: What happened, when, what data was involved, who was affected
3. **Assess**: Client impact, regulatory notification requirements
4. **Remediate**: Correct the issue, revoke access if needed
5. **Report**: File regulatory reports if required (SEC, state regulators)
6. **Update**: Revise this policy to prevent recurrence

## 10. Training Requirements

| Requirement | Timeline | Frequency |
|------------|----------|-----------|
| Initial AI policy training | Within 30 days of policy adoption | One-time |
| Annual refresher | Anniversary of adoption | Annual |
| New hire training | Within first week | One-time |
| New tool training | Before first use | Per tool |

Training covers: approved tools, data classification, human review requirements, incident reporting, and this policy.

## 11. Advisor Intelligence Consultant Access

Christopher Baker (Advisor Intelligence) operates under this policy with the following specific terms:
- Accesses Salesforce through a dedicated user account with appropriate permissions
- All access is logged and auditable
- No client PII is exported to personal systems
- Public SEC data is used for prospecting workflows — no client data involved
- Work product (workflows, configurations, documentation) belongs to DLK
- Engagement governed by separate consulting agreement

## 12. SEC Examination Readiness

This policy is designed to address SEC examination priorities regarding AI:
- **AI in investment advice**: DLK uses AI for operational efficiency, not investment recommendations
- **Marketing Rule compliance**: All AI-generated marketing content undergoes compliance review
- **Cybersecurity**: AI tools are approved and monitored per vendor due diligence
- **Books and records**: AI-generated content retained per existing policy
- **Fiduciary standard**: Human review required before any client-facing output

## 13. Annual Review

This policy is reviewed annually by Don Dempster (CCO) and updated for:
- New AI tools adopted or retired
- Changes in SEC/FINRA AI guidance
- Incidents or near-misses
- Changes in firm processes, personnel, or vendor relationships
- Feedback from team members

All team members acknowledge the updated policy in writing.
