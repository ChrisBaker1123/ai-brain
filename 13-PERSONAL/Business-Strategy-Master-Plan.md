## The Thesis

Advisor Intelligence is not the end goal. It's the trust-building vehicle for a career that ends with me managing wealth. Every phase builds on the last. Every relationship compounds. The person who built your AI tools, installed your AI systems, and proved their value over years is the most trusted person to manage your money.

## Phase 1 — AI Templates & Coaching (NOW)

**Timeline**: 2025–2027
**Vehicle**: Advisor Intelligence (https://www.advisorintelligence.app)

### What It Is
A copy-paste AI template toolkit for financial advisors. 68 templates across 8 workflow categories, plus documents, tutorials, action plans, and an AI Coach chatbot. Advisors copy templates, paste into Microsoft Copilot, get compliance-aware output.

### Why It Matters
- Builds **name recognition** in the advisor community
- Builds **trust** through consistent, high-quality deliverables
- Builds **relationships** with individual advisors and firms
- Creates a **client base** that knows me, likes me, and trusts my judgment
- Positions me as **the person who understands both technology and finance**

### Current State (March 2026)

- 1 active client (Will Jones, Wells Fargo)

- 3 Morgan Stanley contacts in pipeline
- $49.99/mo pricing ready (Stripe configured, not enforced yet)

### Success Criteria
- [ ] 50+ active users
- [ ] $5K+ MRR
- [ ] 3+ firm-level adoptions
- [ ] Case study published (ideally DLK)
- [ ] Recognized as "the AI guy" in San Diego advisor community

## Phase 2 — AI Architecture Installations (NEXT)

**Timeline**: 2027–2029 (overlapping with late Phase 1)
**Vehicle**: Consulting / implementation services (entity TBD)

### What It Is
Installing AI systems — Claude bots, OpenClaw deployments, custom automations — directly into businesses. Not SaaS, but infrastructure. Build it, install it, maintain it.

### Why It Works
- Phase 1 builds the relationship. Phase 2 monetizes the trust.
- Advisors who use my templates already trust my AI judgment
- Natural upsell: "You've been using my templates manually. Let me install a system that does this automatically for your entire firm."
- Higher contract value: $5K–$50K per installation vs. $50/mo per seat
- Stickier relationship: installed systems create dependency and ongoing maintenance

### Target Clients
1. **Advisor firms from Phase 1** — they already trust me
2. **Small/mid businesses** — need AI but can't afford a CTO
3. **Professional services** — law firms, accounting firms, medical practices (same playbook as advisors)

### What I'll Build
- Custom Claude-powered chatbots for client-facing communication
- CRM-integrated automation (auto-generate meeting prep, follow-ups, emails)
- Internal knowledge bases with AI search
- Workflow automation for document generation, compliance review, reporting

### Success Criteria
- [ ] 5+ installations completed
- [ ] $100K+ annual revenue from installations
- [ ] Repeatable playbook for new industry verticals
- [ ] At least 2 clients converted from Phase 1 relationships

## Phase 3 — Financial Advisor (END GOAL)

**Timeline**: Age 25 (approximately 2029–2030)
**Vehicle**: RIA registration or wirehouse affiliation (TBD)

### What It Is
Become a registered financial advisor. Manage wealth for the people I've been serving with AI tools. The ultimate trust conversion.

### Why It Works
- "I already built and installed your AI systems. You trust me. Now let me manage your wealth."
- By 25, I'll have 5+ years of relationships with advisors and firms
- The people I've served will have seen me deliver consistently
- My understanding of both technology and finance will be fully validated
- Young advisor with AI expertise = differentiated positioning in a market of 60-year-olds

### The Trust Funnel
```
Phase 1: Advisor tries my templates → trusts my AI judgment
Phase 2: Advisor hires me to install AI systems → trusts my technical ability
Phase 3: Advisor lets me manage their wealth → trusts me completely
```

Each phase deepens the trust. By Phase 3, the ask isn't "trust a stranger with your money" — it's "trust the person who's been helping you succeed for years."

### Requirements
- [ ] Series 65 or Series 66 + state registration
- [ ] Determine structure: independent RIA vs. join existing firm vs. wirehouse
- [ ] Build minimum AUM to sustain a practice
- [ ] Exit or transition Advisor Intelligence (sell, automate, or delegate)

### Strategic Advantage
- I'll be one of the youngest advisors with deep AI integration
- I'll have a built-in client pipeline from Phases 1 and 2
- The "built your AI systems" backstory is a moat no other advisor can replicate
- Dad (Anthony Baker) is a 30-year Morgan Stanley veteran — warm introductions and industry credibility

## Dependencies Between Phases

```
Phase 1 → Phase 2: Trust earned through templates converts to consulting contracts
Phase 2 → Phase 3: System installations create deep relationships that convert to AUM
Phase 1 → Phase 3: Every advisor interaction builds the network for future client acquisition
```

Phases can overlap. Phase 2 can start while Phase 1 is still running. Phase 3 preparation (Series 65 study, etc.) can start during Phase 2.

---

## Key Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Phase 1 product isn't useful enough | Continuous template quality improvement, direct advisor feedback |
| Phase 2 AI architecture commoditizes | Build relationships, not just systems — the trust is the moat |
| Phase 3 takes longer than expected | Keep Phase 2 revenue running as bridge income |
| Market shifts (AI regulation, advisor tech changes) | Stay adaptive, pivot messaging not mission |
| Biggest fear: product isn't worth the price | Obsess over quality, gather feedback early, don't launch pricing prematurely |
