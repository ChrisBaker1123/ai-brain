## Principle
Acknowledge regulatory reality without fearmongering and without overpromising. Templates reference SEC/FINRA/Reg BI requirements, but never claim to provide compliance approval. The distinction is critical: we help advisors work within compliance frameworks, we don't replace compliance departments.

## Derived From
- [[be-the-same-person-everywhere]] — Honest about what we are and aren't
- [[trust-before-money]] — Overpromising on compliance would destroy trust

## How It Applies
Every template includes compliance references. Marketing uses "compliance-aware" language. Never say "guaranteed," "proven," "risk-free," or "compliance-approved." The trust center explains what we do and don't do clearly.
