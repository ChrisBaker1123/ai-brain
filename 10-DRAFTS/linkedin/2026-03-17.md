## Post 3: "How to Turn Meeting Notes Into a Follow-Up Email in 90 Seconds" (Educational How-To)

**Format**: Text with step-by-step walkthrough
**Schedule**: Thursday 3/20, 7:00 AM PT
**Hook**: Specific, time-bound outcome

---

Advisors spend 41% of their work week on administrative tasks.
(Source: Copytalk/FPA Study)

Meeting follow-ups are one of the biggest time sinks — and one of the easiest to fix with AI.

Here's how to turn your meeting notes into a polished client follow-up email in under 90 seconds using Microsoft Copilot:

**Step 1: Capture the basics (15 sec)**
After your meeting, jot down:
→ Key topics discussed
→ Action items (yours and the client's)
→ Next meeting date
→ Any emotional context (retirement anxiety, market concerns, etc.)

**Step 2: Use a follow-up template (10 sec)**
Instead of staring at a blank prompt, use a structured template that includes:
→ Client name and meeting context
→ Summary of discussion points
→ Clear next steps with deadlines
→ Warm, professional tone matching your practice

**Step 3: Paste into Copilot and review (45 sec)**
Copy the filled template into Copilot.
Read the output once. Adjust any details.
That's it — you've got a follow-up email that sounds like you wrote it.

**Step 4: Send within 2 hours (20 sec)**
Clients notice when you follow up fast. A same-day follow-up after a review meeting signals attentiveness.

No client data enters the AI platform. You're copying a template, not uploading a file.
