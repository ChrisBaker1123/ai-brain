## Decision
Position Microsoft Copilot as the primary AI tool advisors paste templates into. Not ChatGPT, not Claude. Templates say "paste into Copilot."

## Context
ChatGPT has 35.71% market share among advisors, Copilot has 12.12% (T3 Survey). But wirehouse and IBD firms increasingly mandate Copilot through enterprise Microsoft 365 agreements. Copilot is the compliance-friendly choice.

## Rationale
Copilot alignment signals we understand enterprise advisor workflow. Compliance officers prefer Microsoft's enterprise data handling over consumer AI tools. This positions us as the workflow layer for the tool their firm already approves.

## Implications
- Landing page, templates, and tutorials reference Copilot first
- "Works with Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI assistant"
- Templates are designed to be tool-agnostic but marketed Copilot-first
