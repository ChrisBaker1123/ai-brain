## Decision
Display Christopher's phone number (619) 851-2215 on the landing page hero, about page, trust center, pricing page, footer, and contact page. Real person, real number, real availability.

## Context
Advisor feedback: they want to talk to a human, not fill out a form. The personal touch differentiates from VC-funded competitors with support tickets and chatbots.

## Rationale
Advisors manage trust for a living. A real phone number says "I'm a real person who stands behind this product." No funded competitor offers founder-direct support.

## Implications
- Phone number visible on 6+ pages
- Cri must be available to answer (or return calls promptly)
- As the user base grows, this may need to transition to a support system
