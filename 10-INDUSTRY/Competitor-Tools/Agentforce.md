# Salesforce Agentforce for Financial Services

> Last Updated: 2026-03-24. Sources: Salesforce, ZenML, Vantage Point, Eesel AI.

## Overview

Salesforce's native AI agent platform for Financial Services Cloud. Generally available since Summer 2025. Pre-built templates for financial advisor use cases including meeting prep, agenda generation, and post-meeting summaries.

## Pricing

| Option | Per User/Month | Annual (11 users) |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| FSC Enterprise (base) | $325 | $42,900 |
| + Agentforce Industries add-on | $150 on top | $19,800 |
| **Total** | **~$475** | **~$62,700** |
| Agentforce 1 Edition | $750 | $99,000 |
| Flex Credits alternative | $500 per 100K credits | Variable ($0.10/action) |

### Implementation Costs

- $2,000–$6,000 per agent template setup
- Salesforce consultants: $150–$250/hour (ShellBlack, Vantage Point)
- Typical implementation: $25,000 (small RIA) to $200,000+ (mid-size)

## Pre-Built Templates

- **Financial Advisor Assistance**: Meeting prep, agenda creation, post-meeting summaries
- **Client Service Agent**: Answer client questions, surface account information
- **Compliance Assistant**: Review communications, flag potential issues

## Strengths

- Native Salesforce integration (no middleware needed)
- Enterprise-grade security and compliance
- Backed by Salesforce's massive R&D budget
- Pre-built for financial services
- Data stays within Salesforce ecosystem

## Weaknesses vs Advisor Intelligence

- **Extremely expensive** — $62,700/year for 11 users vs our $17,500/year total
- **Requires Enterprise+ edition** — Many RIAs on Professional or with overlays
- **Complex implementation** — Needs Salesforce admin expertise and consultant hours
- **Salesforce-only** — Doesn't help with tools outside SF (Schwab, meeting notes, prospecting)
- **No human support model** — It's software, not a consulting relationship
- **Overkill for most RIAs** — Designed for enterprise, not $350M shops

## Positioning Against

"Agentforce is powerful but costs $63K+/year and only works inside Salesforce. We build a complete AI practice for $17.5K/year — including the Salesforce automation Agentforce would do, plus everything outside SF."
