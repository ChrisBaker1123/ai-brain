## Who Mark Is

**Mark Lindner** is the Director of the Fowler Scholars Program and a Lecturer in the Marketing Department and Sports MBA Program at SDSU's Fowler College of Business. He assumed directorship on May 23, 2022, succeeding Peter Shaw (the original director since the program's 2017 inception).

### Professional Background
Before SDSU, Mark spent 35+ years as a General Manager at Molson Coors Beverage Company (formerly MillerCoors). He developed long-term strategic go-to-market strategies for 10+ million case wholesaler operations. He's a recognized expert in personal branding, brand marketing, and distributor relationship management.

He and his wife Debbie created the **Mark and Debbie Lindner Internship Scholarship** within the Fowler College — their first gift came in 2015, before he even joined the faculty. He also runs **Lindner & Associates** (consulting).

Mark counts Ron Fowler (the program's namesake and founder) as a personal mentor. He has a 5/5 rating on Rate My Professors.

## How We Met

High school. I showed Mark what I was doing with sneaker bots. Not as a pitch — just showing someone what I was building. He was impressed.

But what impressed him wasn't the sneakers. It was the systems thinking. A high school kid running automated purchasing operations across multiple platforms, managing proxies and servers, optimizing for millisecond advantages, scaling a resale business with real revenue. That's not normal for a teenager. Mark saw the potential behind the operation.

That impression led directly to my admission to the Fowler Scholars program at SDSU.
