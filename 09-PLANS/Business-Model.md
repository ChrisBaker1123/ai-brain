# Business Model — AI Consulting for Financial Advisors

> **Effective**: 2026-03-16
> **Catalyst**: Dan Skiles mentorship conversation
> **Core insight**: "The real business isn't the AI toolkit. It's Cri as a consultant who prevents financial advisors from falling behind on AI."

---

## Offer

**What we sell**: Monthly AI consulting retainer for financial advisors. Cri comes in, audits their AI setup, interviews their workflows, gives tips and recommendations, and keeps them current. Monthly. Ongoing. Human relationship.

**What we don't sell**: AI software. A toolkit subscription. A chatbot. The toolkit still exists and still has value — but it's an included resource within the consulting relationship, not the product.

**The analogy**: In the early tech era, businesses didn't hire full-time IT departments. They brought in someone periodically who checked the systems, fixed problems, and gave recommendations. That's what Advisor Intelligence is for AI.

### Pricing Psychology

- **3 tiers** (decoy effect): Tier 2 is the anchor. Tier 1 exists to make Tier 2 look like better value. Tier 3 exists for firms that want premium.
- **$997 is the Carson benchmark**: Matches the industry standard for advisor coaching, establishing credibility by association.
- **Annual discount (20%)**: Incentivizes commitment, improves cash flow predictability.
- **Launch pricing**: First 5 clients get 25% off any tier for 6 months in exchange for testimonial + case study rights.

## The Moat (Why Cri, Why Now)

1. **Bilingual**: Speaks AI fluently. More importantly, speaks advisor. This is what no AI platform, no chatbot, and no generic tech consultant can replicate. Most tech founders don't understand finance. Most financial advisors don't understand tech. Cri understands both.

2. **Insider**: Grew up in the industry. Dad is a 30-year Morgan Stanley advisor. Understands compliance, workflow, culture, the dinner-table conversations about difficult clients and market volatility. This isn't learned from research — it's lived experience.

3. **Young**: Sees AI natively, not as a foreign technology. Advisors hire Cri for the same reason companies hire young people to run their social media — native fluency with the tool.

4. **Personal**: Real phone number (619-851-2215). Real human. Real relationship. Not a chatbot. Not a vendor. A trusted person who picks up the phone.

5. **Low cost**: Compared to a full-time AI hire ($150K+) or big consulting firm ($500+/hour), Cri is a fraction of the cost with more relevant expertise.

6. **Built the tools**: Already built a 68-template AI toolkit for advisors. Demonstrated the ability to create practical AI solutions. The toolkit IS the portfolio — it proves Cri can deliver.

## What This Changes About Phase 1-2-3

### Old Phase 1 (2025-2027): AI Templates & Coaching
Sell a $50/month SaaS toolkit. Build name recognition.

### New Phase 1 (2026-2027): AI Consulting Retainer
Sell monthly consulting relationships ($497-$1,997/month). Build deep trust through ongoing human relationships. Toolkit included as resource.

### Phase 2 (2027-2029): Unchanged
AI architecture installations. Natural upsell: "You've been getting my monthly advice. Let me install the systems."

### Phase 3 (2029-2030): Unchanged
Become a financial advisor. "I've been your AI consultant for years. You trust me. Let me manage your wealth."

**The consulting model accelerates the trust funnel**: Monthly calls build deeper relationships faster than a $50/month SaaS product ever could. Each conversation is a trust deposit. By the time Phase 2 arrives, the relationship is already deep enough to sell $5K-$50K installations.
