## Rule
Never suggest Advisor Intelligence replaces eMoney, Holistiplan, Riskalyze, Orion, Black Diamond, or any planning/analysis/portfolio/risk/compliance software. Always use "complements," "fills the gap between," "handles what your other tools don't."

## Why
Advisors rely on these systems. Suggesting replacement triggers defensiveness and signals ignorance. The gap-filler position is the differentiator — losing it means competing with $363M+ in funded competitors.

## Enforcement
Strict. Applied to all marketing copy, landing page sections, demo scripts, outreach emails, and content drafts.
