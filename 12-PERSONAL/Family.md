## How Family Shapes the Business

The family dynamic is central to understanding why I am the way I am:

1. **Dad = industry access**. I didn't cold-approach this industry. I grew up in it. The bilingual advantage — speaking tech and finance — exists because Anthony Baker raised me in finance while I taught myself tech.

2. **Mom = values**. The "never fabricate, never exaggerate, honest above everything" approach to [[Brand-Voice|brand voice]] comes directly from how I was raised.

3. **Financial security = freedom to build**. I didn't start Advisor Intelligence because I needed money. I started it because I saw an opportunity. That's a different energy than desperation, and it shows in the product. There's no urgency to cut corners.

4. **Maya = stability**. Having a relationship that doesn't depend on the business succeeding means I can make decisions based on what's right, not what's desperate.
