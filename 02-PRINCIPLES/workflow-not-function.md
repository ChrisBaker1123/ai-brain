## Principle
Templates should be organized by what advisors DO during their day (meeting prep, follow-ups, emails, prospecting) not by financial planning functions (estate planning, tax planning, retirement). Planning functions have dedicated software. Workflow tasks don't.

## Derived From
- [[gap-filler-positioning]] — The gap is in workflow, not in planning
- [[when-someone-needs-help]] — Advisors need help with daily tasks, not annual analyses

## How It Applies
The 8 categories reflect advisor workflow: meeting_prep, follow_up_crm, client_emails, client_education, prospecting_growth, content_creation, new_client_onboarding, practice_operations. All marketing language should use workflow terms ("meeting prep," "follow-up emails") not planning terms ("tax planning," "retirement analysis").
