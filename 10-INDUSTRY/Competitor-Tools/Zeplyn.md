# Zeplyn

> Last Updated: 2026-03-24. Sources: Zeplyn website, G2, industry reviews.

## Overview

AI meeting assistant purpose-built for financial advisors. Compliance-first positioning with 95%+ accuracy claims. Known for "Agent Nexus" feature (multi-agent orchestration).

## Pricing

| Tier | Price |
|------|-------|
| Standard | ~$60/seat/month |
| Premium | ~$120/seat/month |
| DLK cost (5–11 seats) | $300–$1,320/month |

## Features

- Meeting recording and transcription (95%+ accuracy claimed)
- AI-generated summaries and action items
- CRM integration (Salesforce, Redtail, Wealthbox)
- Agent Nexus: multi-agent AI for complex tasks
- Compliance-first design (record retention, audit trails)
- Email draft generation
- Task auto-creation from meeting content

## Strengths

- Compliance-first positioning appeals to CCOs like Don
- High accuracy claims
- Agent Nexus is technically interesting
- Purpose-built for advisors (not generic)

## Weaknesses vs Advisor Intelligence

- Single-purpose tool (meeting capture only)
- No workflow automation beyond CRM data entry
- No Salesforce Flow building, prospecting, or practice-wide transformation
- Tool, not consulting relationship
