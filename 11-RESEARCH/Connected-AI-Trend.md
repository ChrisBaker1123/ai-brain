# Connected AI Trend — Industry Analysis

> The advisory industry is shifting from standalone AI tools to connected AI platforms that integrate directly with CRMs, email, custodial data, and meeting transcripts.

## Executive Summary

The connected AI trend is real, accelerating, and has $363M+ in recent VC funding. Major platforms like Altruist (Hazel, $152M Series F) and Morgan Stanley (16,000 advisors) are now deploying AI systems that synthesize client data across multiple sources.

**However, connected AI solves a different problem than Advisor Intelligence.** Connected AI platforms synthesize existing data (what's already in the CRM, email, accounts); Advisor Intelligence creates the communication that advisors send every day. These are complementary, not competitive. Templates get *better* with connected data — advisors can feed richer context to prompts, producing more personalized output.

Our position: **We're the communication layer of the future connected tech stack.** As platforms like Hazel connect data, templates will connect to those platforms to enrich output. This is a long-term moat, not a threat.

## Morgan Stanley — The Wirehouse Template

**Scale**: 16,000+ financial advisors across 500+ branches

**The Product**:
- OpenAI-powered AI assistant available to all advisors
- **"Debrief" feature**: Advisor records Zoom meeting → AI auto-generates CRM notes + draft follow-up emails
- **Compliance integration**: All output reviewed for regulatory compliance before advisor sends
- **Internal-only**: Closed system, no third-party access, meets wirehouse security requirements

**Key Details**:
- Deployed starting 2025, expanded 2026
- Integration with Morgan Stanley's internal systems (CRM, email, compliance workflow)
- Advisors report 20-30 min/day savings on meeting admin
- Regulatory-favorable because: advisor remains in the loop, no external data exposure

**Source**: Financial Planning magazine (2025), Morgan Stanley press releases, Insider reports

**Why This Matters to Us**:
Wirehouses (Morgan Stanley, Merrill Lynch, Wells Fargo, UBS) employ ~50% of all advisors. They *cannot* use external connected AI platforms like Hazel due to compliance restrictions. Morgan Stanley's approach (internal tools + copy-paste workflow) validates our TAM advantage.

## Altruist / Hazel — The Custodian AI Platform

**Funding & Status**:
- $152M Series F (2025), $380M+ total funding
- Launched "Ask Hazel" (Sept 2025), Tax Planning module (Feb 2026)
- 7,000+ advisors, 250+ RIA firms

**The Product**:
"Hazel" = AI assistant that connects to:
- **CRM**: Wealthbox, Redtail (read-only access)
- **Email**: Gmail, Outlook integrations
- **Custodial data**: Schwab, Fidelity, Charles Schwab
- **Meeting transcripts**: Video/audio upload
- **Tax documents**: 1040s, K-1s (via upload or custodian sync)

Advisors ask: "What's the tax impact of this client's Roth conversion?" → Hazel queries all connected data, synthesizes, answers in 10 seconds.

**Tax Planning Module** (Feb 2026):
- Analyzes client tax returns + portfolio + market conditions
- Triggers alerts for planning opportunities (e.g., "This client should harvest losses before year-end")
- Caused brief Schwab/LPL stock sell-offs because advisors suddenly realized they'd been ignoring tax-loss-harvesting opportunities
- Pricing: $60-125/seat/month (negotiated per firm)

**Key Advantages**:
- Solves the "data puzzle" — integrates info scattered across 5-10 systems
- Works for ANY firm, not just Altruist customers
- Deep custodial data access (other platforms can't match this)
- Regulatory compliance built in (audit trails, read-only access)

**Key Limitations** (Our Moat):
1. **No content creation** — Can't write prospecting emails, cold outreach, LinkedIn content
2. **No marketing templates** — No "client education" materials, webinar scripts, practice documents
3. **No follow-up workflows** — No "meeting prep" sequences, nurture campaigns, client appreciation
4. **No practice operations** — No SOP templates, hiring docs, team agendas
5. **Tax-focused, not advisory-focused** — Doesn't solve 90% of advisor communication needs

## Jump / Zocks / VRGL — Meeting AI Wave

**Jump (VRGL)**:
- 27,000 advisors, $80M Series B
- Product: AI records Zoom meetings, transcribes, extracts action items, syncs to Redtail/Wealthbox CRM
- Recent: Just announced portfolio comparison feature
- Limitation: No custodial data, no email/marketing

**Zocks**:
- Privacy-first (doesn't record audio), $45M Series B
- Product: Advisor takes notes during meeting → AI transcribes and structures into CRM
- Limitation: No data synthesis, no custodial access

**Common Thread**:
- Both integrate with CRMs (meeting notes are the entry point)
- Neither connects to email, custodial data, or marketing workflows
- Neither creates communication content

## CRM Vendors Adding AI

**Wealthbox**:
- Modern RIA CRM (150+ integrations)
- Adding AI features: meeting notes auto-tagging, client segmentation, workflow triggers
- Goal: Make CRM smarter, not build connected AI platform

**Redtail**:
- Legacy RIA CRM (market leader by install base, 8,000+ firms)
- Slow to add AI, but recently launched "Redtail AI" for basic meeting notes + follow-up suggestions
- Very limited connected data access

**Salesforce FSC**:
- Enterprise CRM with Einstein AI
- Used by larger RIAs (100+ advisors) and wirehouses
- Powerful data synthesis, but *inside* Salesforce only — no external integrations

**Trend**: CRMs are becoming AI-native, but focused on data management and workflow automation, NOT content creation. They synthesize data for internal use; they don't help advisors communicate externally.

## The Agentic AI Wave

**What It Means**: AI that takes action (not just answers questions). Example: "Review all Roth-eligible clients and draft conversion letters for those likely to benefit."

**Industry Adoption**:
- 44% of finance teams planning agentic AI deployment in 2026 (Industry surveys)
- Advisors are *skeptical* of full automation — they want: "expedite in a non-automated way — make it fast, don't automate it for me" (Kitces, 2026)

**How Templates Fit**:
Templates are the "agentic lite" model. AI generates a draft → advisor reviews → advisor sends. No risk of rogue automation, full advisor oversight maintained.

## Regulatory Landscape

**SEC 2026 Exam Priorities**:
- "Reviewing firm claims about AI capabilities for accuracy"
- No final AI-specific rules yet, but emphasis on explainability and recordkeeping
- FINRA requirement: "Firms must assess compliance obligations BEFORE deploying GenAI"

**Key Risk**: Connected AI platforms handle more data (custodial, email, transcripts) = higher regulatory burden = more audit trails needed = higher risk for advisors

**Our Advantage**: Copy-paste templates keep advisor responsibility clear. No data retention, no audit trails needed beyond what advisors already maintain.

## How This Affects Advisor Intelligence

### Our Position: Communication Layer

**The Gap**:
- Connected AI platforms solve the **DATA** problem — synthesizing what's in CRM, email, accounts, transcripts
- **Nobody** solves the **COMMUNICATION** problem — creating the follow-up emails, prospecting messages, client education materials, marketing content

**The Opportunity**:
Advisors will have better data. Templates will turn that data into better communication.

### Templates + Connected AI = Better Together

**Today (2026)**:
- Advisor manually adds context to template
- Template produces good output
- Example: "My client Sarah just inherited $500K. Template: [Inheritance-Ready Conversation] → Output: conversation script"

**Tomorrow (2027+)**:
- CRM provides context automatically (via integration)
- Template produces great output
- Example: Advisor opens template → CRM auto-fills Sarah's name, inheritance date, current portfolio → template produces personalized script immediately

**Key Insight**: Templates are the constant; context source is the variable. As context sources improve (connected CRM → better context), template output improves automatically.

### What Hazel Doesn't Do (Our Moat)

Hazel can answer: "What's this client's tax situation?" or "What are this client's retirement milestones?"

Hazel cannot create:
1. **Prospecting emails and cold outreach** — Research, topic, angle, CTA
2. **LinkedIn content and social media** — Posts, carousels, hashtags, scheduling
3. **Client education materials** — Webinar scripts, blog posts, animated explainers, PDF guides
4. **Practice documentation** — SOPs, team agendas, hiring job descriptions, client onboarding flows
5. **Client appreciation and relationship nurturing** — Birthday emails, milestone messages, personalized notes
6. **Content creation** — Blog posts, newsletters, video scripts, podcast topics

These are *daily* tasks for advisors. Hazel doesn't touch them.

### The Wirehouse Opportunity

**Why Wirehouses Matter**:
- Wirehouses (Morgan Stanley, Merrill Lynch, Wells Fargo, UBS) = 50% of all advisors (~200K)
- Compliance restrictions prevent use of external connected AI (Hazel)
- Wirehouses build internal tools (like Morgan Stanley's AI assistant)
- Copy-paste templates work *inside* firm security perimeter with Copilot

**This TAM is inaccessible to Hazel** because:
- Compliance requires internal tools only
- External platforms can't access custodial data
- Wirehouses already have internal systems

**Our TAM Advantage**:
- We work for RIAs (independent) AND wirehouses (connected)
- Hazel works for RIAs only
- This TAM difference persists for 5+ years (compliance barriers)

## Strategic Positioning Evolution

**Current (2026)**: "Gap filler" — copy-paste templates for what systems don't cover
- Messaging: "What your CRM doesn't do: templates do it."
- Competitors: ChatGPT (generic), prompt libraries (unstructured)
- TAM: All advisors not yet using connected AI (90%+)

**Next (2027)**: "Communication layer" — templates enriched by optional CRM connections
- Messaging: "Better data → better communication. Templates + your CRM = personalized output in seconds."
- Competitors: Hazel (data layer), general-purpose AI, prompt libraries
- TAM: All advisors with modern CRMs who want better communication

**Future (2028+)**: "Communication layer of the connected tech stack" — works with any AI platform
- Messaging: "Templates work with Hazel, Morgan Stanley AI, or Salesforce. Bring your own data source."
- Competitors: Hazel + templates competitors, proprietary wirethouse systems
- TAM: All advisors, all firms, all data sources
