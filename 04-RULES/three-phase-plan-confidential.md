## Rule
The 3-phase plan (AI coaching → AI architecture installations → financial advisor) never appears in public content, marketing, outreach, or conversations with prospects. It stays in the vault. The people who need to know, know. Everyone else sees the results.

## Why
Premature exposure of the end goal (becoming a financial advisor) could undermine Phase 1 trust. Advisors want to buy from someone committed to the product, not someone using it as a stepping stone. The plan is strategic positioning, not public messaging.

## Enforcement
Strict. Vault-only. Dan Skiles and other mentors do not know the full plan.
