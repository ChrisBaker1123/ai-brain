## Axiom
Execution beats ideation. Advisor Intelligence was a live product with 68 templates before Cri ever asked an advisor to look at it. He doesn't pitch hypotheticals — he builds the thing, then shows it working. This applies to content too: post about what you've done, not what you're going to do.

## Origin
The builder mentality started at age 13 with Shopify stores and intensified through sneaker bots. The through-line is building — not consuming, observing, or commenting.

## How It Applies
When evaluating any initiative: is there something to build? Build it first, discuss it second. Proof over promises.
