## Post 3: "How to Build a Client Newsletter in 3 Minutes with AI" (Educational How-To)

**Format**: Text with step-by-step walkthrough
**Schedule**: Thursday 4/2, 7:00 AM PT
**Hook**: Content creation workflow — least-covered category, high demand

---

Your clients want to hear from you more often. You know this.

But writing a monthly newsletter from scratch? That's an hour you don't have.

Here's how to create one in 3 minutes using Microsoft Copilot and a structured template:

**Step 1: Pick your topic (30 sec)**
What are clients asking about this month?
→ Market conditions
→ Tax law changes
→ Interest rate updates
→ Estate planning basics

You don't need to be original. You need to be relevant and timely.

**Step 2: Fill in the template inputs (60 sec)**
A good newsletter template prompts you for:
→ Firm name and advisor name
→ Topic and key talking points (2-3 bullets)
→ Tone (educational, conversational, or formal)
→ Any specific client segment (retirees, young accumulators, business owners)
→ CTA (schedule review, attend webinar, read blog post)

**Step 3: Paste into Copilot and generate (30 sec)**
The structured input produces a newsletter that sounds like your firm — not a generic AI blog post. Review it. Adjust one or two sentences to add your personal touch.

**Step 4: Send via your existing email platform (60 sec)**
Paste into Mailchimp, Constant Contact, or whatever you use. Add your header and footer. Hit send.

Your clients get consistent communication. Your firm looks proactive. You spent 3 minutes.

No client data entered the AI. You filled in a template, copied the output, and personalized it. That's the copy-paste approach.
