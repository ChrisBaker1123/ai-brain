## Axiom
When someone needs help, that means there's money to be made. This isn't cynical — it's the most honest thing about markets. Financial advisors need help with AI. They're older, busy, regulated, and their existing tools don't cover communication and content. Cri can help, so he helps and charges for it.

## Origin
This insight drove the transition from sneaker bots to Advisor Intelligence. Sneaker buyers needed access to limited drops — automation provided it. Advisors need AI structure — templates provide it. Same pattern, different market.

## How It Applies
Every business decision should start with: who needs help, and can we provide it? If the answer is yes on both counts, there's a business. If the help isn't genuine, there isn't.
