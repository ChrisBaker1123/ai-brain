## {{FIRM_NAME}} — Artificial Intelligence Usage Policy

**Effective Date**: {{date}}
**Last Reviewed**: {{date}}
**Approved By**: {{cco_name}}, Chief Compliance Officer
**Next Review**: {{annual_review_date}}

### 1. Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for the use of artificial intelligence tools at {{firm_name}} to ensure compliance with SEC and FINRA regulations, protect client data, and maintain the firm's fiduciary standard.

### 2. Approved AI Tools

| Tool | Approved Use | Restrictions |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Microsoft Copilot (M365) | Email drafting, document summarization, task management | No client PII in prompts |
| {{additional_tools}} | {{approved_uses}} | {{restrictions}} |

**Adding new tools**: Any new AI tool must be reviewed by {{cco_name}} and documented in this policy before use.

### 3. Prohibited Uses

AI tools shall NOT be used to:
- Make investment recommendations without advisor review and judgment
- Send client communications without human review and approval
- Process, store, or transmit: client Social Security Numbers, account numbers, passwords, full dates of birth, or account balances
- Generate marketing materials without compliance review
- Replace the firm's fiduciary obligations or professional judgment

### 4. Data Classification

| Classification | Examples | AI Usage |
|----------------|----------|----------|
| **Restricted** | SSNs, account numbers, passwords | Never enter into AI |
| **Sensitive** | Client names, financial situations, account types | Use with caution, first names only, no identifying combinations |
| **Internal** | Firm processes, templates, workflows | Permitted with approved tools |
| **Public** | Market data, regulatory text, published research | Freely usable |

### 5. Human Review Requirements

| Activity | Review Required By | Before |
|----------|-------------------|--------|
| Client email or letter | Sending advisor | Sending |
| Marketing content | CCO or compliance designee | Publishing |
| Financial plan summary | Preparing advisor | Client delivery |
| Compliance document | CCO | Filing or distribution |
| Internal workflow output | Workflow owner | Acting on results |

### 6. Record Retention

- AI-generated content that becomes a client communication: retain per the firm's existing Books and Records policy
- AI prompts and queries used for client work: retain for minimum {{retention_period}} months
- AI tool access logs: maintain for audit trail
- This policy and all revisions: retain permanently

### 7. Vendor Due Diligence

For each approved AI vendor, the firm documents:
- Company name and contact
- Data handling practices (where data is processed, stored, retained)
- Security certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, etc.)
- Terms of service review date
- Subprocessor list
- Incident response and notification commitments

Vendor reviews are conducted annually or upon material change.

### 8. Incident Response

If a data breach or misuse of AI tools is suspected:
1. Immediately notify {{cco_name}}
2. Document the incident: what happened, when, what data was involved
3. Assess client impact
4. Report to regulators if required
5. Update this policy to prevent recurrence

### 9. Training

- All team members complete AI usage training within 30 days of this policy's adoption
- Annual refresher training required
- New hires trained within first week
- Training covers: approved tools, data classification, human review requirements, incident reporting

### 10. Annual Review

This policy is reviewed annually by {{cco_name}} and updated for:
- New AI tools adopted or retired
- Changes in SEC/FINRA AI guidance
- Incidents or near-misses
- Changes in firm processes or personnel

All team members acknowledge the updated policy in writing.
