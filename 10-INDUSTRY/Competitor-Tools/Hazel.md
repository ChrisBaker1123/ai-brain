# Hazel by Altruist

> Last Updated: 2026-03-24. Sources: Altruist, Business Wire, InvestmentNews, Wealth Management, RIABiz.

## Overview

AI-powered advisor productivity platform by Altruist (custodian). Acquired from Thyme (Y Combinator). Launched September 2025. Combined custodian + AI play. Caused a $130B selloff in wealth management stocks at launch announcement.

## Pricing

| Tier | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| Base | $60/seat/month | Meeting recording, transcription, summaries, CRM sync, email drafting, daily digest, Ask Hazel chat |
| With Tax Planning | $125/seat/month | All above + reads 1040s, paystubs, account statements, generates tax strategies |
| DLK cost (11 seats) | $660–$1,375/month | Depends on tier |

## Traction

- 1,600 RIA firms subscribed within one month of launch (March 2026)
- Growing fast due to custodial data advantage
- Free for Altruist custody clients (some features)

## Features

- **Meeting recording and transcription** — Real-time capture
- **AI summaries** — Structured meeting notes
- **CRM sync** — Salesforce, Wealthbox, Redtail integration
- **Email drafting** — Post-meeting follow-up generation
- **Daily digest** — Morning briefing of client activity
- **Ask Hazel** — Chat interface for advisor questions
- **Tax planning module** — Reads financial documents, generates strategies

## Strengths

- Deep custodial data integration for Altruist custody clients
- Low price point for what you get
- Vertical integration (custody + AI = seamless data flow)
- Fast growth and strong product velocity
- Zero data retention agreements with AI providers (compliance selling point)
- Compliance module planned (not yet released)

## Weaknesses vs Advisor Intelligence

- **Schwab-custodied firms get ~80% functionality** but miss portfolio intelligence (DLK is on Schwab)
- **No Salesforce workflow automation** — captures data but doesn't build the operational layer
- **Tool, not consultant** — No one helps you figure out what to automate or how to change processes
- **No compliance policy delivery** — Planned but not yet available
- **No meeting preparation** — Only captures, doesn't prepare
- **No prospecting intelligence** — Not in scope
- **One tool** — We integrate across the entire practice; Hazel is one piece

## Positioning Against

"Hazel is great for meeting capture. We help you build the entire operational system around it — including deciding whether Hazel is the right tool for your firm."
