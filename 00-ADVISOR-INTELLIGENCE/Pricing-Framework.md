# Pricing Framework

## Standard Tiers (Website)

| Tier | Monthly | Annual (if offered) | Target Firm |
|------|---------|---------------------|-------------|
| AI Essentials | $497 | $4,970 (2 months free) | Solo advisor or 2-person team |
| AI Partner | $997 | $9,970 | 3-6 advisors, custom workflows |
| AI Architect | $1,997 | $19,970 | 7+ advisors, full integration |

## Custom Engagement Pricing (Fractional AI Officer)

Used when the scope goes beyond standard tiers — system builds, Salesforce integration, M&A intelligence, etc.

| Component | Range | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Setup/onboarding fee | $2,000–$5,000 | Covers audit, interviews, first 3 workflows |
| Monthly retainer | $1,000–$2,500 | 1 workflow/month + support + updates |
| Project add-ons | $500–$2,000 each | One-off builds outside retainer scope |

## Negotiation Parameters

| Parameter | Floor | Target | Stretch |
|-----------|-------|--------|---------|
| Setup fee | $1,500 | $2,500 | $5,000 |
| Monthly retainer | $750 | $1,250 | $2,500 |
| Payment terms | Net 30 | Net 15 | Prepaid quarterly |
| Cancellation | 30-day notice | 30-day notice | 90-day notice |

**Hard floor**: Don't go below $750/month. Below that, the time investment doesn't justify the engagement.

## ROI Justification

### For DLK specifically:
- 6 advisors at 1:78 client ratio, highly capacity-constrained
- Don said: "We have money, we don't have time"
- 10% efficiency gain = ~24 hours/week saved firm-wide
- At $200/hr advisor cost = $19,200/month value
- $1,250/month = **15:1 ROI**

### General pitch:
- "What's the cost of a missed compliance filing?" → $50K+ SEC fine
- "What's the cost of losing a $1M client because review was late?" → $10K+ annual revenue
- "What's the cost of hiring a junior analyst full-time?" → $60K+ salary + benefits
- AI consulting at $1,250/month = $15K/year vs $60K+ for a hire
