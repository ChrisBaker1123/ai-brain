### On DLK's Growth Strategy

Don explained four growth channels for RIAs: (1) client referrals, (2) cold outreach to new prospects, (3) recruiting advisors from other firms, and (4) M&A — acquiring firms whose principals are aging.

Don's focus is on #4: identifying independent RIAs that invest in individual securities (a dying breed — "there aren't very many of us left"), owned by individuals (not trusts or PE), with aging principals approaching succession.

The pitch to targets: "We're not a private group from New York who just want to buy your firm and kick you out. We're actually a local firm that knows a lot of your clients and we're part of the same infrastructure."

### On Salesforce

Mark described wanting automatic task generation when a new prospect is created: disclosures, billing setup, model assignment, newsletter, calendar reminders, welcome email. Plus a dashboard showing pipeline status.

For existing clients: review cycle tracking (last review, next review), automatic draft emails at 60 days, escalation at 30 days, pre-meeting briefs, post-meeting structured updates.

### On Pricing and Structure

Cri proposed $2,500 setup + ~$1,250/month. Don asked the right questions about deliverables and structure. Mark asked about the IT firm analogy — whether ad-hoc calls are included in the retainer (yes).

Don's negotiation style: "Fair is not important. Fair is what's negotiated." He didn't push back on price.

### On the Team

Mark outlined roles: Don = compliance/CEO/strategy, Ted = research, Brian = trading, Tom and Mark = client-facing CFPs. Each person has different AI needs. Cri should interview each person individually.
