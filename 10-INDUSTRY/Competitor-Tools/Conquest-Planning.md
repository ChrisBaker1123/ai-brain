# Conquest Planning

> Last Updated: 2026-03-24. Sources: Conquest Planning website, industry reviews.

## Overview

AI-powered financial scenario modeling tool. Creates visual "what-if" scenarios for client meetings. Not a meeting capture tool — it's a planning visualization and recommendation engine.

## Pricing

| Option | Price |
|--------|-------|
| Monthly | $200/month |
| Per plan | $45/plan |

## Features

- AI-generated scenario modeling
- Visual "what-if" comparison charts
- Retirement, tax, estate, and insurance scenarios
- Client-facing presentation generation
- Integrates with financial planning data

## Relevance to DLK

Low — DLK doesn't do comprehensive financial planning (they're investment managers who do individual stock portfolios and some planning). More relevant for CFP-heavy firms.
