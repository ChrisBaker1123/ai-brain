# Zocks

> Last Updated: 2026-03-24. Sources: Zocks website, InvestmentNews, industry reviews.

## Overview

AI note-taking tool for financial advisors with a key differentiator: **no audio recording**. Uses real-time transcription without storing raw audio. $65M in total funding. Compliance-first positioning makes it ideal for firms uncomfortable with recording client conversations.

## Pricing

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Per seat/month | $100+ |
| DLK cost (5 advisors) | $500+/month |

## Features

- Real-time meeting transcription (no audio recording stored)
- AI-generated summaries
- CRM sync (Salesforce, Redtail)
- Compliance-safe design (no recordings to manage)
- Action item extraction

## Why Recommended for DLK

- Don is compliance-focused ("trust and verify"). No-recording = no audio compliance risk.
- In-person meetings common at DLK. Zocks works on phone/tablet without mounting a recording device.
- Lower compliance burden than tools that record and store audio files.
- Alternative for in-person: Otter.ai on phone.

## Strengths

- No audio recording = compliance-first (strongest selling point)
- Well-funded ($65M)
- Purpose-built for advisor meetings
- Good for firms with compliance-sensitive culture

## Weaknesses vs Advisor Intelligence

- Single-purpose tool (meeting notes only)
- More expensive than Hazel ($100+ vs $60/seat)
- No Salesforce automation, prospecting, or workflow building
- Accuracy questions (no recording = real-time only, no re-processing)
