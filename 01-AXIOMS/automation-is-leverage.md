## Axiom
Technology multiplies human effort. One person with the right bot can outperform a thousand refreshing manually. One advisor with the right templates produces better output faster than one without. Whoever has better tools wins.

## Origin
Learned through sneaker botting — milliseconds determined success. The kids who bought sneakers manually lost. The kids with better tools won. Same pattern in every industry.

## How It Applies
Every product feature should multiply the user's output. If a template doesn't save measurable time, it's not providing leverage.
