# DLK SEC Exam Readiness — AI Usage

> How DLK's AI adoption aligns with 2026 SEC examination priorities.

## SEC 2026 Examination Priorities Related to AI

### 1. Use of AI in Investment Advice
**SEC concern**: Firms using AI to generate or influence investment recommendations without adequate oversight.

**DLK position**: ✅ Strong
- AI is used for **operational efficiency** (onboarding, reviews, prospecting), not investment advice
- Portfolio decisions remain with Ted Kay, Brian Johnson, and the advisory team
- No AI tool has access to trading systems or investment models
- Human review required before any client-facing output

### 2. Marketing Rule Compliance
**SEC concern**: AI-generated marketing materials that contain misleading claims, unsubstantiated performance, or testimonials.

**DLK position**: ✅ Strong
- All marketing content (including AI-generated drafts) undergoes CCO review before publication
- AI Usage Policy explicitly requires compliance review for marketing materials
- No AI tool auto-publishes any marketing content

### 3. Cybersecurity and Data Privacy
**SEC concern**: AI tools creating new attack surfaces, data leakage to third-party AI vendors.

**DLK position**: ✅ Strong
- Approved tool list with vendor due diligence on each
- Data classification framework (Restricted/Sensitive/Internal/Public)
- No client SSNs, account numbers, or restricted data entered into AI tools
- Microsoft Copilot operates within M365 tenant boundary
- Prospecting system uses only public SEC data

### 4. Books and Records
**SEC concern**: AI-generated content not properly retained or attributed.

**DLK position**: ✅ Strong
- AI-generated content that becomes client communication: retained per existing Books and Records policy
- AI prompts retained for 12 months
- Access logs retained for 24 months
- All record types documented in AI Usage Policy

### 5. Vendor Due Diligence
**SEC concern**: Firms adopting AI tools without vetting vendors for security, data handling, and conflicts.

**DLK position**: ✅ Strong
- Vendor Due Diligence document maintained for each approved tool
- Annual review cycle
- New tools require CCO approval before use

## Exam Preparation Checklist

If SEC examiners ask about AI:

- [x] Written AI Usage Policy in place (deliver Week 1)
- [x] Approved tool list with vendor documentation
- [x] Data classification framework
- [x] Human review requirements documented
- [x] Training program for all staff
- [x] Record retention policy for AI-generated content
- [x] Incident response plan
- [ ] Staff acknowledgment signatures (collect after policy adoption)
- [ ] Annual review completed (first review after 12 months)

## Key Message for Examiners

"DLK adopted AI tools under a formal policy framework approved by our CCO. We use AI for operational efficiency — automating routine tasks, improving client communication workflows, and research on public data. All investment decisions and client-facing communications require human review and approval. No restricted client data enters any AI system. Our vendor due diligence and record retention practices are documented."
