## Rule
Never fabricate user counts, testimonials, or social proof. No "Join 500+ advisors" when there are 4 users. No fake quotes. No manufactured urgency. No "trusted by top advisors nationwide" when there's one active client. When real social proof exists, use it with attribution.

## Why
Fake social proof destroys trust permanently. Advisors manage trust for a living — they can spot manufactured credibility instantly. Getting caught fabricating even once would end the business.

## Enforcement
Strict. Claude Code agents are instructed never to generate fabricated counts or testimonials. All social proof must be verifiable.
