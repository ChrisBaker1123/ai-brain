## Post 3: "How to Create a Client Welcome Email in 2 Minutes with AI" (Educational How-To)

**Format**: Text with step-by-step walkthrough
**Schedule**: Thursday 3/26, 7:00 AM PT
**Hook**: Specific task, specific time — new client onboarding

---

You just signed a new client. Congratulations.

Now you need to send a welcome email that:
→ Sets expectations for the relationship
→ Outlines next steps clearly
→ Feels warm and professional — not templated
→ Reflects your firm's voice

Most advisors either copy-paste something generic or write it from scratch every time. Neither is great.

Here's how to do it in 2 minutes with Microsoft Copilot:

**Step 1: Gather your inputs (30 sec)**
→ Client name
→ Type of engagement (comprehensive plan, investment-only, etc.)
→ First meeting date
→ Any specific goals they mentioned in the prospect meeting

**Step 2: Use a structured onboarding template (15 sec)**
A good template prompts you for:
→ Firm name and advisor name
→ Service tier and scope
→ Key deliverables in the first 90 days
→ Preferred communication cadence
→ Contact information and scheduling link

**Step 3: Paste into Copilot and generate (30 sec)**
Copy the filled template into Copilot. Review the output.
Adjust the tone if needed — you know your client.

**Step 4: Send and set the tone (45 sec)**
A strong welcome email within 24 hours tells your new client: "You made the right decision."

No client data enters the AI platform. You're copying a template and pasting the output — nothing is stored.
