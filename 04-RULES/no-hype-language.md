## Rule
Never use "guaranteed," "proven," "risk-free," "#1," "best," "revolutionary," "cutting-edge," or fear-based urgency ("Don't get left behind!"). Never use "AI-powered" as a feature — the templates ARE the product.

## Why
Hype language destroys credibility with an audience that manages trust for a living. Compliance-conscious advisors immediately distrust superlatives. Educational, specific language builds real trust.

## Enforcement
Strict. All marketing copy, blog posts, LinkedIn posts, and email sequences must pass this filter.
