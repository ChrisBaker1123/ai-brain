## Axiom
Money is a high priority, but the goal is legacy, not lifestyle. The kind of wealth that compounds across generations, not the kind that looks good on Instagram. This informs everything from product pricing (fair, not extractive) to investing (long-term physical AI, not meme stocks) to how success is discussed (honest, not performative).

## Origin
Family upbringing — grew up comfortable but grounded. Colleen Baker's influence on values.

## How It Applies
Pricing should feel fair. Marketing should feel honest. The product should genuinely help, not just look impressive.
