# Retention Strategy — How They'll Miss Us When We're Gone

> **The test**: If an advisor cancels after 3 months because they don't notice a difference, we failed. If an advisor panics at the thought of losing their monthly call because they'd be lost without it, we succeeded.
>
> **Source question**: Dan Skiles — "How will they miss us when we're not there?"

---

## The Honest Risk

The biggest retention threat is that Month 1 delivers the audit and roadmap (high perceived value), but by Month 4-5 the advisor feels "I got what I needed" and questions the ongoing cost. This is the classic consulting churn pattern.

**The fix**: Engineer 10 specific "miss us" moments into the engagement — things the advisor depends on that disappear the moment they cancel.

---

## 10 "Miss Us" Moments

### 1. The Practice Intelligence File (Accumulated Knowledge)

What: A private document about their practice that grows monthly. Client segments, AUM tiers, compliance quirks, CRM setup, tech stack, communication preferences, which templates work best for which client types.

Why it sticks: After 6 months, Cri knows their practice better than any replacement could learn in under a year. A new consultant starts from zero. This is the #1 switching cost. (Fractal Analytics runs 114% net revenue retention specifically because of accumulated institutional knowledge.)

Miss us moment: They realize the next consultant would need 6 months just to get to where Cri already is.

### 2. The Living Roadmap (Evolving Document Dependency)

What: A "Practice AI Roadmap" updated monthly. Tracks: what's implemented, what's next, what's on the horizon in AI for advisors, and how it maps to their specific practice.

Why it sticks: The roadmap is their strategic plan for AI. If they cancel, the roadmap stops evolving. The AI landscape changes monthly — a static roadmap is stale in 60 days.

Miss us moment: A major AI update drops and they have no one translating what it means for their practice.

### 3. Monthly AI Impact Brief (ROI Reset)

What: 1-page monthly report showing: templates used, estimated time saved, new templates added, AI news relevant to their practice, next month's focus.

Why it sticks: Every 30 days, the value question gets answered with hard numbers. "You saved approximately 8 hours this month" makes the $997 feel like a bargain.

Miss us moment: They cancel and no one is quantifying the value of AI in their practice anymore. The vague feeling of "is this working?" returns.

### 4. The Quick-Call Trusted Advisor Status

What: Unlimited quick calls (under 15 min) included in every tier. Not a support ticket. A text or call to a real person.

Why it sticks: The advisor develops a habit of texting Cri when they have a question about AI, tech decisions, or workflow problems. This is the behavioral hook. The more they rely on quick answers, the harder it is to operate without that access.

Miss us moment: They hear about a new AI tool from a conference, and for the first time there's nobody to text "should I be using this?"

### 5. Proactive Industry Intelligence

What: Monthly curated "AI in Wealth Management" briefing. Regulatory changes (SEC on AI), new tools (Copilot updates), competitor moves (what other RIAs are doing with AI), action items for their practice.

Why it sticks: This is their window into a fast-moving space they don't have time to track. Cri filters signal from noise.

Miss us moment: They see an AI headline and realize they have no one interpreting what it means for them.

### 6. Workflow Embedding

What: Don't just hand advisors templates — embed Cri's work into their daily operations. Set up Copilot configurations, customize templates to their client segments, build their prompt library inside their existing tools, train their staff.

Why it sticks: The more Cri's work is woven into daily operations, the more painful extraction becomes. Replacing Cri means retraining staff and rebuilding workflows.

Miss us moment: Their admin tries to create a new client email and realizes all the templates were calibrated to their voice and compliance requirements by someone who's no longer there.

### 7. Quarterly Business Reviews (Formal Value Ceremonies)

What: 90-minute deep session every quarter. Review AI adoption progress, time/money saved, new capabilities, competitive positioning, next-quarter priorities.

Why it sticks: Creates a cadence the client builds their planning around. Produces a paper trail of documented value.

Miss us moment: Q2 arrives and nobody has scheduled the strategic review they've come to rely on.

### 8. Progressive Template Customization (Compounding Personalization)

What: Start with 68 generic templates. Each month, customize 2-3 specifically for their practice — their compliance language, client personas, tone, product set.

Why it sticks: By month 6, they have a bespoke prompt library no competitor can replicate. By month 12, a fully personalized AI system.

Miss us moment: They try a generic tool and realize how much calibration work they've lost.

### 9. Client Peer Network (Future, 5+ Clients)

What: Once we have 5+ clients, create a private quarterly roundtable where clients share AI wins (anonymized). Peer learning that only Cri's clients get access to.

Why it sticks: Network effect. The value of the community grows with each member.

Miss us moment: They lose access to what other advisors are doing with AI.

### 10. Scope Expansion (The De Facto Technology Advisor)

What: Start with AI templates, then naturally extend into: tech stack recommendations, CRM workflow optimization, client communication strategy, marketing ideas, practice efficiency.

Why it sticks: Every new domain touched is another thread that's hard to pull out.

Miss us moment: They need advice on their next tech purchase and realize their AI consultant was also their technology advisor.

## Retention Timeline

| Month | Key Retention Hooks Active |
|-------|---------------------------|
| 1 | Audit + roadmap delivered (high initial value), toolkit access granted |
| 2 | First quick wins implemented, Practice Intelligence File growing, first monthly brief |
| 3 | QBR #1 with ROI data, quick-call habit forming, 2-3 custom templates built |
| 4-5 | **DANGER ZONE** — proactive industry intel and roadmap updates must demonstrate ongoing value here |
| 6 | Deeply embedded in workflows, bespoke template library, second QBR |
| 9 | Third QBR, scope has expanded, client can't imagine going back |
| 12 | Annual review, full ROI documented, relationship is autopilot |

## How the Website Should Communicate This

The top 3 "miss us" moments that should be visible on the website:

1. **"The AI landscape changes monthly. I keep you current."** (Already on homepage — good)
2. **"Custom recommendations for YOUR practice"** — implies accumulated knowledge (Already on homepage — good)
3. **"On-demand support — phone, email, text"** — the quick-call trusted advisor (Already on homepage — good)

**What's missing from the website**: The concept of the "living roadmap" and the monthly AI brief. These are high-value retention hooks that should be explicitly named on the pricing page.
