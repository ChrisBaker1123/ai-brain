# Jump AI

> Last Updated: 2026-03-24. Sources: Jump.ai, Wealth Management, Financial Planning, XYPN, InvestmentNews, T3 2026 Survey.

## Overview

AI productivity platform for financial advisors. Founded as Jump Technology, rebranded. Raised $105M total ($80M Series B). 27,000+ advisors. Enterprise clients include Focus Financial (800 advisors), LPL, Osaic. Market leader by share.

## Market Position

- **22.68% market share** (T3 2026 Survey) — up from 8.36% in 2025
- Fastest-growing advisor AI tool by adoption
- Enterprise-first go-to-market, now moving downstream

## Pricing

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Per advisor/month | $75–$100 |
| DLK cost (5 advisors) | $375–$500/month |
| DLK cost (11 seats) | $825–$1,100/month |
| Enterprise pricing | Custom (volume discounts) |

## Features

- **Pre-meeting prep automation** — Key differentiator. Pulls data from CRM, financial planning tools, estate planning, portfolio management
- **Schwab custodial data integration** — Reads account data, balances, positions
- **12+ CRM integrations** including deep Salesforce integration
- **Salesforce specifics**: Reads contacts/activity/client data, writes notes/tasks/updates back via OAuth
- **Meeting transcription and summaries**
- **Client communication drafting**
- **Portfolio analytics** (via VRGL acquisition)

## Strengths

- Largest market share and momentum
- Deep data integrations (pulls from 12+ systems)
- Enterprise-proven (Focus Financial, LPL scale)
- Pre-meeting prep is genuinely useful (our research confirms this is high-value)
- $105M funding = strong product development runway
- Schwab custodial data access (relevant for DLK)

## Weaknesses vs Advisor Intelligence

- **Not plug-and-play** — XYPN review: 3.5/5 on raw transcription accuracy, requires tuning
- **Tool, not transformation** — Automates existing processes but doesn't redesign workflows
- **No Salesforce automation building** — Reads/writes data but doesn't build Flows or automate operational processes
- **No compliance infrastructure** — Doesn't deliver AI usage policies or vendor due diligence
- **No prospecting intelligence** — Not in their feature set
- **No human support model** — Self-service tool, no consulting relationship
- **Price adds up** — $75–100/advisor/month × 5 advisors = $375–$500/month for ONE tool. We deliver full-practice consulting for $1,250/month.

## Positioning Against

"Jump is one of the best advisor AI tools on the market. We'd probably recommend it as part of your stack — and then build everything else around it that Jump doesn't do."
