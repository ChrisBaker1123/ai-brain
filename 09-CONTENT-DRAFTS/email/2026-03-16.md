## Email

**From**: Christopher Baker <christopher@advisorintelligence.app>
**Subject**: "Is this compliant?" — here's the honest answer
**Preview text**: How our templates handle the compliance question

---

**Body:**

Hi [FirstName],

Every advisor I talk to asks the same question within the first five minutes:

"Is this compliant?"

Here is the honest answer: we are compliance-aware, not compliance-approved. And that distinction matters.

**What compliance-aware means:**

Every template in the toolkit includes references to relevant regulatory considerations. If a template generates client-facing language, it flags where your compliance team should review. If a template touches on investment performance or forward-looking statements, the output includes guardrails that avoid the language regulators flag.

We researched SEC marketing rule updates, FINRA communication guidelines, and common compliance review triggers to build these references into every template.

**What compliance-approved does NOT mean:**

We are not a compliance tool. We do not provide legal advice. Every output should be reviewed through your firm's existing compliance process before it reaches a client.

**Why this approach works:**

Most AI tools for advisors ignore compliance entirely. You get a polished email that sounds great — until your compliance officer redlines half of it.

Our templates are built so the AI output arrives closer to compliant from the start. Less redlining. Fewer revision cycles. Faster time to send.

**The data advantage:**

Because templates are copy-paste, client data never enters our platform. Your compliance review for Advisor Intelligence is straightforward: no data processing agreement needed, no vendor security assessment, no third-party risk evaluation on our side. The only review is the AI output itself — which you are already reviewing through your existing process.

**See it in action:** [Browse Templates with Compliance References](https://www.advisorintelligence.app/toolkit)

Tomorrow I will show you how other advisors are tracking their time savings — and whether this is actually worth it.

Christopher Baker
Advisor Intelligence

## Design Notes

- **Layout**: Plain text email with one blue CTA button ("Browse Templates with Compliance References")
- **Length**: ~250 words body (slightly longer than usual — compliance needs explanation)
- **Tone**: Transparent, direct, no hedging. Advisors respect honesty about limitations.
- **Personalization**: [FirstName] from `full_name.split(" ")[0]`
- **Single CTA**: Toolkit page (/toolkit) — let them see compliance references in context
- **Subject line**: Uses quotes to create curiosity gap. "Is this compliant?" is the exact question advisors ask.
- **Key phrase**: "compliance-aware, not compliance-approved" — this is a brand line, use it exactly
