## Production Notes

- **Frequency**: Monthly, delivered by email by the 5th
- **Length**: 1 page (500-700 words max)
- **Personalization**: Each client gets a customized version referencing THEIR tools, THEIR roadmap, THEIR practice
- **Source material**: AI news aggregation, vendor release notes, SEC/FINRA notices, client toolkit analytics
- **Time to produce**: ~30 min per client once the template and news scan are done. Batch the news section, personalize the practice section.
