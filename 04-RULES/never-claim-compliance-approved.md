## Rule
Never use "compliance-approved," "guaranteed compliant," "SEC-approved," or any language that implies regulatory endorsement. Always use "compliance-aware" — we reference regulations, we don't provide compliance approval.

## Why
We're a technology vendor, not a registered investment advisor. Claiming compliance approval would be legally risky and immediately destroy trust with any advisor who understands the regulatory landscape. The distinction matters enormously in this industry.

## Enforcement
Strict. Every piece of marketing copy, every template description, every landing page section must be checked for this.
