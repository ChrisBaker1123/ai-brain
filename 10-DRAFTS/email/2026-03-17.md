"How do I make this fit into my actual day?"

That's not something a template can answer. It takes someone who understands your workflows, your client communication standards, and the way your team actually operates.

That's why Advisor Intelligence is a consulting practice — not just a toolkit.

→ **Month 1**: I audit your current setup, identify the 3–5 workflows where AI saves you the most time, and build custom templates for your practice.

→ **Ongoing**: I train your team, refine the workflows as your needs change, and keep you current as AI tools evolve.

→ **Always included**: Full access to the AI Toolkit (68 templates, 8 categories, AI Coach) as a resource for your team.
