# Business Model

> "You don't need another AI tool. You need someone who speaks your language."

## Service Definition

**Fractional AI Officer** for independent RIAs. Cri audits their AI setup, builds custom workflows, trains their team, and keeps them current. Monthly retainer model with setup fee.

The IT analogy: in the early tech era, businesses outsourced IT to someone who came in periodically. That's what Advisor Intelligence does for AI. Per Dan Skiles: "Head of AI is a new division in everybody's company. If you don't have one, you have to buy it, rent it, or hire it."

### Custom Engagement (DLK Model)

| Component | Amount | Includes |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| Setup fee | $2,500 | Full tech audit, team interviews, 3 initial workflows |
| Monthly retainer | $1,250 | 1 new workflow/month, tool updates, training, ad-hoc support |

Custom pricing negotiated per engagement based on:
- Firm size (headcount, AUM)
- Scope of workflows requested
- Complexity of tech stack integration
- Number of team members to support

### ROI Math

For a firm with 6 advisors at 1:78 client ratio:
- 10% time savings = ~4 hours/week/advisor = 24 hours/week firm-wide
- At $200/hr blended advisor cost = $4,800/week saved = ~$19,200/month
- ROI on $1,250/month retainer: **15:1**

## What's Included

### Every Tier
- AI Toolkit access (68 templates, 8 categories)
- 20 documents, 18 tutorials, 20 action plans
- AI Coach chatbot
- Tool recommendations and updates
- Ad-hoc support (call/text/email)

### Custom Engagements Add
- Individual team member interviews (15-20 min each)
- Full tech stack audit (Salesforce, custodian, rebalancing tools)
- Custom workflow builds (Salesforce Flows, Python automations, integrations)
- Compliance documentation (AI usage policies, vendor due diligence)
- Monthly deliverable reports
- Training sessions (group and individual)

## Target Market

**Primary**: Independent RIAs, $150M–$750M AUM
- Employee-owned or principal-owned firms
- 3–15 advisors
- Using Salesforce, Redtail, or Wealthbox as CRM
- Custodied at Schwab, Fidelity, or Pershing
- Want AI but don't know where to start

**Secondary**: Wirehouse advisors (Wells Fargo, Morgan Stanley, UBS, Merrill)
- Limited to personal coaching only
- Cannot build systems or access firm tools (FINRA/compliance blocks outside consultants)
- Lower revenue per engagement

## What We Don't Do

- Replace financial planning/tax/portfolio software (eMoney, Holistiplan, Riskalyze, Orion)
- Provide investment advice
- Access client PII or account data directly
- Send client communications automatically (always draft → advisor review → send)
- Claim AI replaces advisor judgment

## Competitive Moat

Cri is the product. The human relationship is the value. Specifically:
1. **Bilingual**: Speaks both AI/tech and financial advisor language
2. **Speed**: New tools and workflows deployed monthly, not annually
3. **Compliance-native**: Every workflow built with SEC/FINRA awareness
4. **Personal**: Direct phone line, 24/7 availability
5. **Toolkit included**: 68 templates as a resource, not a separate charge
