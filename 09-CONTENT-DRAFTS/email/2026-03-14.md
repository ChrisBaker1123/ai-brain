## Design Notes

- **Layout**: Plain text email with one blue CTA button ("Open the Document Builder")
- **Length**: ~180 words body (scannable in 30 seconds)
- **Tone**: Helpful peer, not salesy. Showing value, not pushing features
- **Personalization**: [FirstName] from `full_name.split(" ")[0]`
- **Single CTA**: Document Builder page (/documents)
- **Mobile**: Preview text "Create complete client documents in minutes" shows on mobile lock screen
