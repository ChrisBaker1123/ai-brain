# Advisor Intelligence — Overview

#project #overview

## What It Is
Advisor Intelligence is a **monthly AI consulting practice** for financial advisors. Cri Baker audits their AI setup, builds custom workflows, trains their team, and keeps them current as the AI landscape evolves. The consulting relationship includes full access to an **AI Toolkit** — 68 templates, Document Builder, How-To Guides, Action Plans, and an AI Coach chatbot.

The toolkit still operates on a **copy-paste model**: advisors select a template, it auto-injects their profile data, they copy it to their clipboard, and paste it into their firm's approved AI tool. Client data never enters the platform.

## Positioning (updated 2026-03-16)
**"You don't need another AI tool. You need someone who speaks your language."**

The real business is Cri as a consultant who prevents financial advisors from falling behind on AI. The IT analogy: in the early tech era, businesses didn't hire full-time IT departments — they brought in someone periodically to check systems, fix problems, and give recommendations. That's what Advisor Intelligence does for AI.

The toolkit is an included resource within the consulting relationship, not the primary product. See [[Decision-2026-03-16-Pivot-To-Consulting]] and [[Business-Model]].
