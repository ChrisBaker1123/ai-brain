## The Altruist Connection

### What Altruist Is
A modern custodian built for independent RIAs — the tech-forward alternative to Schwab, Fidelity, and Pershing. Founded 2018 by Jason Wenk. Self-clearing brokerage firm.

### Scale & Funding
- **Series F** (April 2025): $152M led by GIC (Singapore sovereign wealth fund)
- **Total raised**: ~$602M
- **Valuation**: ~$1.9B
- **4,700+ advisors** served
- **Market share**: 2.85% → 6.25% (2025 T3 survey) — still fourth behind Schwab, Fidelity, Pershing
- Tripled AUM for two consecutive years

### Hazel AI
Altruist's AI product. An integrated assistant within the Altruist platform that helps advisors with various tasks. It has access to account data, client information, and platform context — a "connected AI" approach.

**How Hazel competes with Advisor Intelligence**: Hazel works within Altruist for Altruist users. It's platform AI.

**How we're different**: Advisor Intelligence works with ANY AI tool (Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude), is platform-agnostic, requires zero data upload, and focuses specifically on communication/content templates. We're the gap-filler. Hazel is the platform AI.

**The complementary angle**: An advisor could use Altruist/Hazel for portfolio operations AND Advisor Intelligence for meeting prep, client emails, and content creation. We don't compete — we complement.

## Strategic Analysis

### Why This Relationship Matters

Dan Skiles is arguably the most strategically valuable mentor in the Fowler network for Advisor Intelligence. Here's why:

1. **He's been at every layer of the custodian stack** — Schwab, SSG, now Altruist. He understands how advisor technology decisions get made at the platform level.
2. **He was named IA 25** — one of the 25 most influential people in the advisor industry. His endorsement carries weight.
3. **He sat on the FPA National Board** — Mark Halby (DLK) is on FPA San Diego's board. That's a shared network.
4. **He wrote "Technology Coach" for ThinkAdvisor** — he understands how to communicate tech to advisors.
5. **He's at Altruist** — which is competing with Schwab/Fidelity for RIA custody. If Altruist grows, and it is growing fast, Dan's influence in the advisor world grows too.

### The Tension
- Altruist's Hazel AI is in the same market as Advisor Intelligence
- If Hazel expands into communication/content templates, the overlap increases
- Sharing competitive strategy with someone at a competitor is risky

### The Strategy
- **Maintain the mentorship relationship** — it's genuinely valuable and not adversarial
- **Learn from Dan's market insight** — he has decades of seeing what works in advisor tech
- **Never position adversarially against Altruist** — always "complement," never "compete"
- **Don't share the 3-phase business plan** — the confidential strategy stays in the vault
- **Frame the relationship as mentee learning from industry veteran** — true and non-threatening
- **If asked about competition**: "We fill the gaps between systems. Altruist handles operations, we handle communication. Different layers of the stack."
- **Long-term play**: If Dan ever leaves Altruist or Altruist pivots away from Hazel, the relationship with Dan becomes even more valuable as a pure industry connection

### The FPA Connection
Dan sat on FPA's National Board (2014–2016). Mark Halby sits on FPA San Diego's board. This is a shared orbit. If Dan knows Mark Halby or DLK through FPA circles, that's an additional warm introduction pathway.
