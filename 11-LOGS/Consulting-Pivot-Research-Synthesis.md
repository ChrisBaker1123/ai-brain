# Consulting Pivot — Research Synthesis

> **Date**: 2026-03-16
> **Catalyst**: Dan Skiles conversation — "The real business isn't the toolkit. It's Cri as a consultant."
> **Research scope**: 8 parallel research agents across coaching models, wirehouse reality, RIA market, solo GTM, pricing, content, DLK pitch, and website design.

---

## 1. Who Is the Real Customer?

### PRIMARY: Independent RIAs ($150M-$750M AUM)

**This is the core market. Everything else is secondary.**

- **~3,200 midsize RIA firms** in the US ($150M-$749M AUM), collectively managing ~$1T
- Average firm: **8-9 employees, ~$365M AUM, $100-180K/year tech budget**
- **63% using AI** but only **10% have it strategically integrated** — 90% are still experimenting
- **Only 50% of their technology is integrated** (Ezra Group data)
- They can make purchasing decisions without corporate compliance approval
- They actively hire outside consultants (tech, compliance, practice management)
- **84% expect tech budgets to increase**; **92% would switch firms over bad tech**

**Why they need Cri specifically:**
- They're overwhelmed by 300+ software options across 48 categories
- They're adopting AI at the individual level, not strategically
- They need someone who speaks both AI and advisor — not a generic tech consultant
- Their biggest pain: "We have all the data we need, but none of it talks to each other"

### SECONDARY: Large RIAs ($750M+ AUM)

- ~1,000 firms, collectively managing ~$3.2T
- More likely to have internal resources, but many still lack AI strategy
- Higher contract value potential
- Longer sales cycles

### LIMITED: Wirehouse Individual Advisors

**The honest assessment: wirehouses are a dead end for consulting.**

- Morgan Stanley: **98% AI adoption** across 20,000 FA teams. OpenAI exclusive partnership. Building "super agents."
- Wells Fargo: **Tachyon** platform, 191 AI projects deployed, Google Agentspace, 4,000 employees through Stanford AI training
- Merrill Lynch: Ask Merrill, Client360, 95 AI-powered event notifications
- UBS: UBS Red, Microsoft Copilot to all employees, saving 10,000 hours/month on meeting prep

**FINRA barriers are near-total:**
- Rule 3270: Advisors cannot hire outside consultants without firm approval
- Rule 3110: All technology must be supervised by the firm
- Off-channel crackdown: $63M in fines in Jan 2025 alone for unapproved tools
- Approved vendor lists require multi-year enterprise sales process

**The only wirehouse play**: Personal productivity coaching for individual advisors through personal relationships — using their own devices, outside firm systems. This is a relationship play (Anthony, Jane, Tim, Will), not a scalable market. Good for early revenue and testimonials. Don't build the business around it.

## 2. What Can We Honestly Offer Wirehouses?

**Limited scope only:**
- Personal AI productivity coaching (email, prospecting, content creation, personal branding)
- General AI literacy — how to better use the tools the firm already provides
- Time management and workflow optimization using personal AI tools
- None of this touches firm data, firm systems, or anything behind the compliance firewall

**What we cannot offer wirehouses:**
- Integration with firm CRM, portfolio systems, or compliance tools
- Custom AI implementations on firm infrastructure
- Anything that requires firm vendor approval
- Firm-wide adoption (this is an enterprise sale we can't make)

**Honest assessment**: Price this as personal coaching ($200-$400/month), not consulting. It's a side revenue stream, not the core business.

### Market benchmarks:
| Comparable | Price | Model |

| Carson Coaching | $995/month (6-month min) | 1:1 executive coaching |
| FP Transitions | $95-$165/month | Membership/subscription |

| F2 Strategy (OCTO) | ~$2,000-$5,000/month | Fractional CTO retainer |
| Free custodian programs | $0 | Value-add from Schwab, Fidelity |

### Recommended pricing (3 tiers):

**Tier 1 — AI Essentials ($497/month)**
- Monthly 30-min check-in call
- AI toolkit access (templates, documents, guides)
- Monthly AI brief (what changed, what matters)
- Email/text support for quick questions
- Best for: Solo practitioners, emerging advisors

**Tier 2 — AI Partner ($997/month) ← ANCHOR**
- Monthly 60-min strategy call
- Comprehensive AI audit (month 1)
- Custom 90-day AI roadmap
- AI toolkit access
- Monthly AI brief + personalized recommendations
- On-demand phone/email/text support
- Quarterly business review
- Best for: Small teams (2-5 advisors), growing firms

**Tier 3 — AI Architect ($1,997/month)**
- Bi-weekly 60-min calls
- Full AI audit + implementation support
- Team training sessions (monthly)
- Custom workflow design
- All Tier 2 features
- Priority support
- Best for: Multi-advisor firms (5+), firms in active growth

**Launch pricing**: 20-30% discount for first 5 clients in exchange for testimonials and case study rights. First 1-2 clients could be free/discounted pilots.

## 5. Competitive Landscape for Advisor AI Consulting

**Direct competitors in AI consulting for advisors: essentially none.**

The existing players fall into adjacent categories:

| Player | What They Do | Gap Cri Fills |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| Carson Coaching | Practice management coaching | Not AI-specific |
| Ezra Group | Wealthtech consulting | Enterprise-focused, not hands-on AI |
| F2 Strategy | Outsourced CTO for RIAs | Broad tech, not AI-specialized |
| Schwab AI in Action | Free AI education for Schwab RIAs | Educational only, no implementation |
| Select Advisors Institute | Practice management consulting | Not AI-focused |
| Generic AI consultants | AI implementation for businesses | Don't understand advisor workflows |

**The gap**: No one is doing hands-on, advisor-specific AI consulting at the individual/small-firm level. Existing tech consultants (Ezra Group, F2 Strategy) serve larger firms and are adding AI as a feature. Coaching firms (Carson) focus on practice management, not technology implementation. Custodians (Schwab) offer free education but not personalized consulting.

**Cri's positioning**: The only AI consultant who grew up in the advisory industry, understands advisor workflows from the dinner table, and can both build and implement AI solutions. This is the "bilingual" advantage — no one else occupies this intersection.
