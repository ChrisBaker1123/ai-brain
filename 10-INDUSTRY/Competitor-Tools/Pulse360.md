# Pulse360

> Last Updated: 2026-03-24. Sources: Pulse360 website, SmartAsset, industry reviews.

## Overview

Meeting documentation platform for financial advisors. Differentiates on professional deliverables and batch processing. Flat pricing (not per-seat) makes it attractive for larger teams.

## Pricing

| Tier | Price | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Starter | $49/month | Flat (not per-seat) |
| Professional | $99/month | Batch processing |
| Enterprise | $199/month | Full features |
| DLK cost | $99–$199/month | Much cheaper than per-seat tools |

## Key Differentiator

**Flat pricing** — $49–$199/month regardless of team size vs $60–$125/seat at competitors. For DLK's 11 people, this is 5–10x cheaper than Hazel or Zocks.

## Features

- Meeting note templates and standardization
- Batch processing (process multiple meetings at once)
- Professional client deliverables (formatted reports, not raw transcripts)
- CRM integration
- Agenda templates

## Strengths

- Flat pricing is extremely compelling for larger teams
- Professional output quality (polished client-facing documents)
- Batch processing for high-volume days
- Good for standardizing meeting documentation across a firm

## Weaknesses vs Advisor Intelligence

- Documentation-focused only (no real-time transcription, no AI chat)
- No Salesforce automation or workflow building
- No prospecting intelligence
- Tool, not consulting — no one helps implement or optimize
