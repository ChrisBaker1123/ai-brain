# RIA Industry Economics

> Last Updated: 2026-03-24. Sources: Schwab RIA Benchmarking Study, Raymond James 2025, Fidelity, CircleBlack, Kitces Research.

## Market Size

- ~3,500–5,000 SEC-registered RIAs in the $200M–$600M AUM band
- Median SEC-registered RIA manages $393M with 8 employees
- ~3,200 midsize RIA firms ($150M–$749M AUM) collectively managing ~$1T
- ~1,000 large RIAs ($750M+ AUM) collectively managing ~$3.2T

## Revenue Model

| Metric | Typical Range |
|--------|--------------|
| AUM fee | 0.50%–2.00% annually |
| Median fee | ~1.00% for $500K accounts |
| Fee compression | Trending toward 0.75% for larger accounts |
| Billing | Quarterly, in advance or arrears |
| Minimum account | $100K–$500K (varies by firm) |
| Revenue per client | ~$20,000/year for a $350M firm |

## Firm Economics by Size

| AUM | Revenue (at ~1%) | Typical Team | Tech Budget (3.8–5% of rev) |
|-----|-----------------|-------------|------------------------------|
| $100M | $1M | 2–3 people | $38K–$50K |
| $250M | $2.5M | 4–6 people | $95K–$125K |
| $350M | $3.5M | 7–8 people | $133K–$175K |
| $500M | $5M | 8–12 people | $190K–$250K |
| $1B | $10M | 15–25 people | $380K–$500K |

### DLK Profile (~$350M–$435M AUM)

- Revenue: $2.6M–$3.5M annually at 0.75%–1.0% blended fees
- Team: ~11 employees (6 advisors + support)
- Clients: ~470 across 9 states
- Technology budget: $100,000–$175,000/year estimated
- Revenue per client: ~$5,500–$7,400/year (lower than median — larger client count, smaller average)

## Operating Margins

- Operating margins compressed from ~28% (2020) to ~18% (2023)
- Compensation consumes ~74% of total expenses
- Technology costs growing as a percentage of revenue (3.8%–5%)
- 84% of RIAs expect tech budgets to increase

## Typical Team Structure ($250M–$500M Firm)

| Role | Count | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Lead advisors / CFPs | 2–3 | Client-facing, revenue generators |
| Client service associates (CSAs) | 2–3 | Support, scheduling, paperwork |
| Operations manager | 1 | Back office, compliance support |
| Compliance / admin | 1 | CCO duties, often part-time or shared |
| **Total** | **~7** | Many wear multiple hats |

## Growth Challenges

- 51% of advisors cite administrative time as top barrier to growth
- 78% of firms actively hiring, only 42% have succession plans
- 58% of advisors report losing business due to inferior tech
- 92% of clients say they would switch firms over technology shortcomings
- 66% of advisors say firm's technology needs improvement (Advisor360)
- Top shortcomings: lack of AI capabilities (39%), poor integration (37%)
- Only 50% of firm technology is integrated (Ezra Group)

## Growth Channels (from [[DLK-HUB|DLK transcript]])

1. **Client referrals** — #1 source. Service quality → referrals → new assets
2. **Cold outreach** — Hardest. 11 no's for every yes.
3. **Advisor recruitment** — Bring in advisors with their books
4. **M&A / Succession** — Buy firms with aging principals (Don's focus)

## Industry Trends (2026)

- PE rollups accelerating (cheap financing → buy → centralize → resell)
- Aging advisor population (average advisor age ~55, many 70+)
- Individual stock managers becoming rare ("there aren't very many of us left" — Don)
- AI adoption early but accelerating (63% using AI, doubled since 2023)
- Compliance scrutiny increasing on AI use
- 300+ software options across 48 categories overwhelming firms
- Consolidation trend: larger firms acquiring smaller ones for scale

## Time Value Economics

- Advisor time value: $200–$400/hour
- Staff time value: $40–$75/hour
- 520 hours/year on notes and CRM = $78,000–$208,000 in lost productivity per advisor
- 10% efficiency gain at DLK = ~24 hours/week saved firm-wide
- At $200/hr blended = $19,200/month value → 15:1 ROI on $1,250/month retainer
