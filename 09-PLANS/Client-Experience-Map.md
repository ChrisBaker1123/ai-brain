# Client Experience Map — What Advisors Get Month by Month

> Hand this to a prospect: "Here's exactly what the next 6 months look like."

---

## AI Partner Tier ($997/month) — The Core Experience

### Month 1: The AI Audit

**Week 1**
- 60-min kickoff call: I learn your practice, your team, your pain points
- Technology audit questionnaire sent (current tools, workflows, time sinks)
- Toolkit access granted (68 templates, documents, guides)

**Week 2-3**
- I review your responses and analyze your tech stack
- I identify your top 3 quick wins (where AI saves time immediately)
- I map your team's AI maturity (who's experimenting, who's skeptical)

**Week 4**
- 60-min roadmap delivery call
- You receive: **AI Audit Report** (where you stand today)
- You receive: **90-Day AI Roadmap** (what to do next, prioritized)
- First quick win implemented together on the call

**What you have after Month 1**: A clear picture of your AI readiness, a prioritized plan, and at least one workflow that's already faster.

---

### Month 2: Quick Wins

- 60-min strategy call: implement quick wins from the roadmap
- 2-3 templates customized specifically for your practice
- Monthly AI Brief delivered (what changed in AI this month)
- On-demand support: text or call me anytime with questions

**What you have after Month 2**: 3+ workflows running faster with AI. Time savings you can feel. Specific templates tailored to your client types.

---

### Month 3: First Quarterly Business Review

- 90-min QBR: review progress, document ROI, update the roadmap
- Estimated time saved calculated and documented
- Template library now includes 5+ customized templates
- Your Practice Intelligence File is growing (I know your practice deeply)
- Team training session: hands-on workshop for your staff

**What you have after Month 3**: Documented proof of ROI. An updated roadmap. A team that's starting to use AI independently. A consultant who knows your practice inside and out.

**The "would you cancel?" test**: If you cancel now, you lose the roadmap updates, the monthly briefs, the custom templates, and the person you text when you have a question. Most clients don't.

---

### Month 4-6: Deepening

- Monthly strategy calls continue
- Roadmap evolves as AI landscape changes
- Custom templates now covering most of your common client scenarios
- Monthly AI briefs keep you current without effort
- Quick-call habit is established — you text Cri when you need guidance
- Workflows are embedded — your team uses AI templates daily

**What you have after Month 6**: AI is part of your practice operations, not an experiment. 10+ custom templates. A consultant who knows your practice better than any replacement could learn in 6 months. Second QBR documents 6-month ROI.

---

### Month 7-12: Strategic Partnership

- Scope naturally expands: tech stack advice, CRM optimization, marketing ideas
- Third and fourth QBRs document full-year trajectory
- AI maturity has measurably improved (from experimenting → strategic)
- Cri has become a trusted advisor — not just an AI consultant
- Annual review: full-year ROI, Year 2 strategic plan

**What you have after Month 12**: A practice that uses AI strategically. 15+ custom templates. A year of documented ROI. A trusted advisor relationship that's worth more than the monthly fee.

---

## AI Essentials Tier ($497/month) — The Lighter Touch

Same structure but scaled:
- 30-min monthly check-in (vs 60-min)
- No formal AI audit in month 1 (lighter assessment via call)
- No team training sessions
- Email/text support (no priority phone)
- Monthly AI brief included
- Template customization happens more gradually

Best for solo practitioners who need guidance but not heavy implementation.

---

## AI Architect Tier ($1,997/month) — The Full Service

Same structure but amplified:
- Bi-weekly 60-min calls (vs monthly)
- Full AI audit with individual team member interviews
- Custom workflow design for each team member
- Monthly team training (vs quarterly)
- Priority same-day support
- Vendor evaluation and recommendations
- Process documentation and SOPs

Best for multi-advisor firms going through active AI implementation or firm transition.

---

## The Cancellation Conversation

When an advisor says "I think I'm good now":

**Honest response**: "I understand. Let me show you what you'd keep and what you'd lose."

**What they keep**: Everything already built — custom templates, the audit report, any SOPs created.

**What they lose**:
- Monthly AI briefs (the landscape changes monthly)
- The living roadmap (stops updating)
- On-demand support (no one to text with questions)
- Template customization (stops evolving with their practice)
- QBRs and ROI tracking
- Cri's accumulated knowledge of their practice
- Access to future templates and tools as they're built

**The real answer**: "AI tools get smarter every month. Someone still needs to evaluate them for YOUR practice and adapt your workflows. That's what the retainer is. If you're confident you can stay current on your own, you absolutely should cancel. But most advisors find that the monthly check-in is what keeps them from falling behind again."
