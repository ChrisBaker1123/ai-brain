# E&O / Professional Liability Insurance Research

Last Updated: 2026-03-24

## Overview

Errors and Omissions (E&O) insurance — also called professional liability insurance — protects against claims that professional mistakes, negligence, or omissions caused a client financial loss. Essential for Advisor Intelligence given we're consulting with financial firms and accessing their systems. See also [[CA-Licensing-Research]].

## Why It Matters for Advisor Intelligence

1. **Client requirements** — RIA firms commonly require proof of E&O from technology vendors
2. **System access risk** — We access client Salesforce instances, email systems, and configure AI workflows
3. **Advice liability** — If AI workflow recommendations lead to operational issues for a client
4. **Credibility** — Having coverage signals professionalism to prospective clients
5. **Contract requirement** — Larger firms will require proof of insurance before signing

## ERGO NEXT Insurance (formerly Next Insurance)

### Pricing
- E&O policies start at **$19/month** for low-risk businesses
- 73% of customers pay **$45 or less per month**
- IT consultant E&O pricing depends on: type of work, client types, location, employee count, revenue, coverage limits

### Key Details
- **Application**: 100% online, ~10 minutes
- **Certificate of Insurance (COI)**: Instant, no additional cost — can share immediately with clients
- **Coverage**: Claims of professional mistakes, negligence, missed deadlines, not delivering on contracts
- **Bundling**: Up to 10% discount when bundling E&O with other policies

### IT Consultant-Specific Coverage
- System configuration errors causing client downtime
- AI workflow recommendations leading to operational issues
- Data handling mistakes
- Missed project deadlines causing financial loss
- Professional negligence claims

### Additional Coverages Available
- **General Liability (GL)**: Bodily injury, property damage at client sites
- **Cyber Liability**: Data breach response, forensic investigation, notification costs — available as add-on to GL
- **Commercial Property**: Protects equipment (laptops, servers, networking hardware)
- **Business Owner's Policy (BOP)**: Bundles GL + commercial property at discount
- **Workers' Comp**: Required if you have employees in CA
- **Commercial Auto**: If driving to client offices

### Potential Exclusions
- Not found: specific exclusions for financial services consulting
- Standard E&O exclusions typically include: intentional wrongdoing, criminal acts, known prior claims
- **Needs verification**: Whether consulting specifically for RIA/financial firms triggers any exclusions or higher premiums

### The Hartford — Deeper Look
- Average E&O cost: $76/month ($907/year)
- Consultants specifically: ~$96/month
- Specialized **Technology E&O** policy available — combines professional liability with tech-specific coverages
- Strongest claims processing and customer service per MoneyGeek analysis
- Limits up to $10 million
- No online instant quote — must call or work with agent

### Hiscox — Deeper Look
- Policies from ~$22.50-$37/month for select businesses
- $35-$65/month is common range
- Customized policies for 180+ professions
- Covers claims filed anywhere in the world (as long as filed in US)
- Up to $2M limits (higher available in certain situations)
- Online purchase available
- Best for firms with <10 employees

### Technology E&O (Tech E&O) Bundles
Some providers offer **Technology E&O** that combines:
- Standard professional liability / E&O
- Cyber liability insurance
- Media liability
- Network security liability

This bundled approach is ideal for IT/AI consultants who handle client data. Available from The Hartford, Insureon, and others.

## Recommended Approach for Advisor Intelligence

### Phase 1: Immediate (Now)
- **ERGO NEXT (Next Insurance)**: Get E&O coverage online in ~10 minutes
- Estimated cost: $19-$45/month based on solo consultant profile
- Generates instant COI for DLK and other clients
- Add cyber liability as add-on

### Phase 2: As Revenue Grows
- **Hiscox or The Hartford**: Upgrade to higher limits ($1M-$2M)
- Consider Technology E&O bundle (E&O + cyber + network security)
- Estimated cost: $37-$96/month

### Phase 3: Enterprise Scale
- **The Hartford Technology E&O**: Up to $10M limits
- Workers' comp when hiring employees
- Commercial auto if applicable
- SOC 2 compliance (reduces premiums and satisfies client requirements)

## Coverage Recommendations

| Coverage | Recommended Limits | Why |
|----------|-------------------|-----|
| **E&O / Professional Liability** | $1M per claim / $1M aggregate (minimum) | Client contracts will likely require at least $1M |
| **General Liability** | $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate | Standard for vendor agreements |
| **Cyber Liability** | $1M | We access client systems and data |
| **Deductible** | $1,000-$2,500 | Balance premium savings vs out-of-pocket risk |
