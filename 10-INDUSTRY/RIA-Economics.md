# RIA Industry Economics

## Revenue Model

| Metric | Typical Range |
|--------|--------------|
| AUM fee | 0.50%–2.00% annually |
| Median fee | ~1.00% for $500K accounts |
| Fee compression | Trending toward 0.75% for larger accounts |
| Billing | Quarterly, in advance or arrears |
| Minimum account | $100K–$500K (varies by firm) |

## Firm Economics by Size

| AUM | Revenue (at 1%) | Typical Team | Tech Budget |
|-----|-----------------|-------------|-------------|
| $100M | $1M | 2-3 people | $20K-$40K |
| $250M | $2.5M | 4-6 people | $40K-$80K |
| $500M | $5M | 8-12 people | $80K-$150K |
| $1B | $10M | 15-25 people | $150K-$300K |

**DLK at ~$400M**: ~$4M revenue, 11 employees, likely $60K-$100K tech budget

## Time Allocation (Advisor)

Per Kitces research, average advisor time:
- Client meetings: 20%
- Client prep and follow-up: 15%
- Email/communication: 15%
- Administrative tasks: 20%
- Business development: 10%
- Investment management: 10%
- Compliance: 5%
- Other: 5%

**AI opportunity**: Admin (20%) + email (15%) + prep (15%) = 50% of time is automatable

## Growth Channels (from DLK transcript)

1. **Client referrals** — #1 source. Service quality → referrals → new assets
2. **Cold outreach** — Hardest. 11 no's for every yes.
3. **Advisor recruitment** — Bring in advisors with their books
4. **M&A / Succession** — Buy firms with aging principals

## Industry Trends (2026)

- PE rollups accelerating (cheap financing → buy → centralize → resell)
- Aging advisor population (average advisor age ~55, many 70+)
- Individual stock managers becoming rare ("there aren't very many of us left")
- AI adoption early but accelerating
- Compliance scrutiny increasing on AI use
