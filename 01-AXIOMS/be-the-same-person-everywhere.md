## Axiom
No code-switching between "personal brand Cri" and "business meeting Christopher." Trustworthy. Friendly. Intelligent. Professional. Advisors manage trust for a living — they smell inauthenticity from across the room. If Instagram shows one person and the product shows another, trust breaks before it starts.

## Origin
This emerged from the understanding that financial advisors evaluate trust before anything else. Consistency IS trust.

## How It Applies
Every piece of content, every product interaction, every meeting must reinforce: this person is exactly who they appear to be. The "same person everywhere" test: would you say this in a meeting with a $500M advisor?
