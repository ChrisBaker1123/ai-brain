# AI Adoption in the RIA Industry (2026)

> Last Updated: 2026-03-24. Sources: Schwab RIA & AI Research Study (Jan 2026), Seeking Alpha, Accenture, ISS Market Intelligence, Advisor360.

## Headline Statistics

- **63% of RIAs now use AI** — doubled since 2023 (Schwab RIA & AI Research Study, Jan 22, 2026)
- Only **1 in 10** AI-using advisors fully integrate it into business strategy (~6–7% total)
- **82%** rely on generative AI through individual experimentation, not firm-wide systems
- Only **1 in 5** firms have a vision related to AI adoption
- Only **20%** have started employee AI training
- **78%** of RIAs lack written AI policies (ISS Market Intelligence)

## Adoption Barriers

1. **Compliance uncertainty** — "Am I allowed to use this?" 77% concerned about data quality/accuracy (Accenture)
2. **Data privacy** — "What happens to my clients' data?" Trust deficit with cloud AI
3. **Complexity** — "We know it does more, we just don't know how" (Don Dempster, [[DLK-HUB|DLK]])
4. **Time** — "We have money, we don't have time" (Don Dempster, [[DLK-HUB|DLK]])
5. **Integration** — Most tools don't connect to existing tech stack. Only 50% of tech is integrated.
6. **No training** — 44% of compliance professionals have no formal testing of AI outputs
7. **No policy** — 78% lack written AI policies

## Future Outlook

- 59% believe AI will impact client relationships within next year
- 68% expect AI to be transformative within 3 years
- Lisa Salvi (Schwab): "The gap between early adopters and everyone else is widening"
- "2026 is the year of AI agents" — wealth technology experts

## AI Implementation Failure Rates

- **95%** of generative AI pilots fail to deliver P&L impact (MIT NANDA 2025)
- **80%+** of AI projects fail to reach production (RAND Corporation — 2x failure rate of non-AI IT)
- **42%** of companies abandoned most AI initiatives in 2025 (up from 17% in 2024, S&P Global)
- DIY AI implementations succeed ~33% vs vendor-led at ~67% (MIT)
- **53%** of enterprise SaaS licensed seats go unused (Zylo 2025)
- Only **34%** of orgs report positive ROI on most AI projects (Lucid Software)
- HBR March 2026: seven structural frictions preventing AI from scaling
