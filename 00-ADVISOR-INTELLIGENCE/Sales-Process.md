# Sales Process

## Pipeline Stages

1. **Lead** — Name identified, no contact yet
2. **Contacted** — Initial outreach sent
3. **Discovery** — Meeting scheduled or had first conversation
4. **Proposal** — Sent pricing/scope, waiting for decision
5. **Negotiation** — Pricing/scope discussion
6. **Closed Won** — Engagement letter signed
7. **Onboarding** — First month delivery
8. **Active** — Ongoing retainer

## Discovery Meeting Structure (60 min)

### Opening (5 min)
- Personal connection (SDSU, FPA, mutual contacts)
- "I'm not here to sell you an AI tool. I'm here to understand how you work and whether I can save you time."

### Team Mapping (15 min)
- Who does what? What's each person's daily workflow?
- What tools do you use? (CRM, custodian, rebalancing, planning)
- What takes the most time? What falls through the cracks?

### Pain Point Deep Dive (20 min)
- "If you could wave a magic wand and automate one thing, what would it be?"
- "What's the most repetitive task your team does?"
- "When's the last time something fell through the cracks — missed review, forgotten follow-up?"
- "How do you currently prospect for new clients/acquisitions?"

### Demo (10 min)
- Show 2-3 relevant templates from the toolkit
- Show a workflow example (not generic — specific to their pain point)
- "This is what month 1 would look like for your firm"

### Close (10 min)
- Summarize what you heard
- Propose 3 initial workflows
- State pricing: setup + monthly
- "I'll send over an engagement letter this week"

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We don't have time to learn AI" | "That's exactly why you hire me. I learn it, set it up, and train you. You just use it." |
| "We're too small for this" | "You're actually the perfect size. Big enough to benefit, small enough that I can customize everything." |
| "What about compliance?" | "Every workflow I build includes compliance guardrails. I'll draft your AI usage policy as part of onboarding." |
| "AI will replace our advisors" | "AI handles the admin so you can do more advising. It's a junior analyst, not a replacement." |
| "We can do this ourselves" | "You can. But you said yourself — you have money, not time. I compress the learning curve." |
| "That's expensive" | "A junior analyst costs $60K+. I cost $15K and deliver more because I specialize in this." |
