## Axiom
No robots. No wait times. No answering machines. When an advisor has a question, a real person picks up — the person who built the product. This doesn't scale forever, but in the early days, the founder answering every call is an unbeatable advantage no VC-backed company can match.

## Origin
Rooted in the understanding that advisors value personal relationships above all else. They manage trust for a living.

## How It Applies
Personal support is a competitive moat at early stage. Every interaction is a trust-building moment. Never automate human connection when the relationship matters.
