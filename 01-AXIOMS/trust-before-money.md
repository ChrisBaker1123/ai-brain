## Axiom
Advisor Intelligence is not the end goal — it's the trust-building vehicle. The 3-phase career plan works because each phase deepens trust: templates → consulting contracts → wealth management. By Phase 3, the ask isn't "trust a stranger with your money" — it's "trust the person who's been helping you succeed for years."

## Origin
Informed by growing up in a financial advisory household where trust is literally the product.

## How It Applies
Never optimize for short-term revenue at the expense of long-term trust. Free early access builds trust. Honest marketing builds trust. Being the same person everywhere builds trust.
