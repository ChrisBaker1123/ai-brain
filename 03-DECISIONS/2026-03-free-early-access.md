## Decision
Product is free during early access. No credit card required. Stripe is configured for $49.99/mo with 7-day trial but not enforced yet.

## Context
With only 4 users in the database, the priority is adoption and feedback, not revenue. Trust must be established before charging.

## Rationale
Free access maximizes trial volume. Trust is built through usage, not marketing. Early users become evangelists and case studies. Revenue comes after product-market fit is proven.

## Implications
- Stripe paywall exists but isn't active
- Need clear transition plan when pricing activates
- Must track which users are most engaged for conversion targeting
