# FP Alpha

> Last Updated: 2026-03-24. Sources: FP Alpha website, Kitces, XYPN, SmartAsset.

## Overview

AI-powered financial document analysis platform. Reads tax returns, wills, insurance policies, and other documents to identify planning opportunities across 16 disciplines. Not a meeting tool — it's a document intelligence tool.

## Pricing

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Annual subscription | $1,795/year |
| Per month equivalent | ~$150/month |
| DLK cost | $1,795/year (firm-wide) |

## Features

- Reads and analyzes: tax returns (1040), wills, insurance policies, trust documents, estate plans, account statements
- Covers 16 planning disciplines (tax, estate, insurance, retirement, education, etc.)
- Identifies planning opportunities advisors may miss
- Generates client-ready reports
- Integration with financial planning software

## Strengths

- Unique capability (document analysis across 16 disciplines)
- Identifies revenue opportunities (planning gaps = upsell potential)
- Annual flat pricing (predictable cost)
- Complements rather than competes with meeting AI tools
- Could potentially benefit Ted (DLK research)

## Weaknesses vs Advisor Intelligence

- Niche tool (document analysis only)
- No meeting capture, no CRM automation, no prospecting
- Requires advisors to upload and manage documents manually
- Limited to planning opportunity identification — doesn't execute

## Potential Recommendation for DLK

Could be recommended as part of Ted's research toolkit. $1,795/year is reasonable for the unique capability. Would complement, not compete with, our consulting.
