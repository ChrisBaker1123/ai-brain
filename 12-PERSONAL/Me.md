## The Short Version

I'm Christopher Baker — Cri to the people who know me. I'm 20 years old, born and raised in San Diego, studying finance at SDSU as a Fowler Scholar. I'm building [[Overview|Advisor Intelligence]], an AI toolkit for financial advisors, but that's just Phase 1 of a bigger plan. I speak two languages fluently: technology and finance. That's rare, and it's my edge.

My dad is a financial advisor. I grew up around this industry. I understand how advisors think, what they need, and where technology falls short for them. I also understand how to build the technology that fills those gaps. The person who built the product is the best person to help you use it — that's my value proposition, and it's real.

## What Drives Me

I started building things young. Shopify stores in my early teens. No trigger, no pivot moment — just an innate pull toward building things and making money. Then sneaker bots in high school — Bath, Trickle Wrath, Mech — running automation at scale before I could drive. That's where I learned that technology is leverage, speed is advantage, and supply/demand arbitrage is everywhere if you know where to look.

The sneaker era taught me the core lesson: when someone needs help, there's money to be made. Financial advisors need help with AI. I know finance through my dad. I know technology because I've been building since I was 13. I'm the bridge.

## The Plan

Advisor Intelligence is Phase 1. It's the trust-building vehicle.

**Phase 1 (now)**: AI templates and coaching for financial advisors. Build trust, build name, build relationships.
**Phase 2 (next)**: AI architecture installations for businesses. Claude bots, OpenClaw deployments. Sell to the advisors and firms who already trust me.
**Phase 3 (end goal)**: Become a financial advisor. The AI work builds the trust and client relationships. Eventually I manage their wealth.

By 25, I exit Advisor Intelligence and transition to full-time advising. The AI architecture angle is the long-term bridge: "I already built and installed your AI systems, you trust me, now let me manage your wealth."
