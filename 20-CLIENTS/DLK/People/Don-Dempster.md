## What He Wants from AI

### Primary: M&A Prospecting Intelligence System (WF1)

Don's #1 strategic priority. He wants to identify independent RIAs for succession planning / acquisition conversations.

**His criteria** (from transcript):
- RIAs that invest in **individual securities** (not just mutual funds/ETFs) — "There aren't very many of us left"
- Firms **owned by individuals** (not trusts, holding companies, PE-backed)
- Principals aged **75-80** approaching succession
- Geographic focus: **Southern California** first, then expand
- Cross-reference principals against **LinkedIn** and DLK's network
- Find shared connections: nonprofits, board memberships, professional associations (FPA, CFA Society)

**His pitch to targets**: "We're not a private group from New York who just want to buy your firm and kick you out. We're actually a local firm that knows a lot of your clients and we're part of the same infrastructure."

**Two approaches he uses**:
1. **Continuity planning**: "We'll be in the shadows, work with you, and then in five years we'll take over"
2. **Direct acquisition**: "Here's some money. Here's a plan. We'll buy you out."

**System design** (from transcript):
- **Data source**: SEC IAPD (Investment Adviser Public Disclosure)
  - Query all RIAs with $150M–$750M AUM in Southern California
  - Extract principal names and ages
  - Flag firms with individual securities in Form ADV Part 2
  - Filter out PE-backed, trust-owned, holding company structures

- **Intelligence layer**:
  - LinkedIn cross-reference: Find each principal on LinkedIn, extract education, board seats, professional affiliations
  - DLK network map: Cross-reference LinkedIn profile against Mark's FPA connections, Dan Skiles referrals, Cri's network
  - Nonprofit/association overlap: DLK team is on boards of nonprofits, associations — flag shared board membership or event attendance
  - Client overlap: Do target firm's clients overlap with DLK's existing client base? (indicates market knowledge)
  - News: Has principal been mentioned in recent M&A, awards, or industry publications?

- **Delivery format**: Weekly intelligence brief with **10–20 targets ranked by connection strength**
  - Rank 1: Shared board seat or nonprofit membership
  - Rank 2: Same alumni association, professional society, or geographic cluster
  - Rank 3: Peripheral network (mutual LinkedIn connection through FPA, CFA Society)
  - Rank 4: No existing connection, but fits criteria (age, AUM, geography, securities focus)
  - For each target: 1-paragraph approach angle identifying the shared connection

**Why this matters**: Don says RIAs investing in individual securities are "very few" left. DLK already owns 89 individual positions. This is a strategic moat and an M&A filtration system rolled into one.

### Secondary: Compliance Automation — Fee Audit

**The exact workflow** (from transcript):
1. Download client list with fees from billing system
2. Cross-reference against signed advisory contracts (fee schedules)
3. For each client, verify:
   - Does the contracted fee match billed fee?
   - Account for non-billable assets (inherited stock, concentrated positions DLK doesn't manage)
   - Is any client being over/under-billed?
4. Quarterly automated report to Don's inbox flagging discrepancies

**Why this matters**: "We are highly, highly regulated. The SEC is going to come in and say 'You told them 50 basis points but you charged them 60 basis points.'"

**Delivery**: Quarterly compliance audit report (email to Don) showing:
- All clients, contracted fee, actual fee, variance
- Accounts over/under-billed
- Non-billable asset reconciliation
- Signed by Cri confirming completeness
