## Rule
Personal details about Maya, family financial information, investment positions, and private relationship details inform content but never appear in public output. Client details (specific AUM figures, strategies, private conversations) require explicit permission.

## Why
Privacy is non-negotiable. The vault is the private brain; the website and content are the public face. Some information exists to inform decisions, not to be published.

## Enforcement
Strict. Claude Code agents are instructed never to leak vault-only information into public content.
