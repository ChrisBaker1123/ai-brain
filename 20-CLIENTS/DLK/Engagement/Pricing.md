# DLK Pricing

## Agreed Terms

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Setup fee | $2,500 |
| Monthly retainer | $1,250 |
| Year 1 total | $17,500 |

## Context from Negotiation

- Cri proposed: $2,500 setup + $1,250/month (stated as "$1,205" in transcript, likely verbal approximation)
- Don's response: "Fair is not important. Fair is what's negotiated."
- Don didn't push back on pricing — focused on deliverables and structure
- Don asked the right questions: "What is in the setup? How do you get it into your boat?"

## What's Included

### Setup ($2,500)
- Full tech stack audit (Salesforce, Schwab, redblack, M365)
- Individual team member interviews (6 people × 15-20 min)
- AI Usage Policy draft for compliance review
- 3 custom workflows built and deployed
- Group training session
- Month 1 deliverable report

### Monthly ($1,250)
- 1 new workflow or significant enhancement
- Tool updates and recommendations
- Salesforce maintenance
- Unlimited ad-hoc support (phone/email/text)
- Monthly deliverable report
